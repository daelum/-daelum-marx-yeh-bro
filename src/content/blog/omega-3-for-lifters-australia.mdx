## TL;DR

- Omega-3 (EPA/DHA from fish oil or alternatives) is a **basics** lane — not a muscle drug or detox.
- Buy on **EPA + DHA mg per serve**, not “1000 mg fish oil” vanity.
- Run **AUD per combined EPA+DHA**, including AU shipping.
- Seed CTA: [/go/omega-iherb](/go/omega-iherb).

## Label maths that matter

Front-of-bottle “1000 mg fish oil” can mean **much less** EPA+DHA.

```
combined_epa_dha_mg = EPA_mg + DHA_mg (per softgel or serve)
AUD_per_1000mg_epa_dha ≈ (price_AUD_incl_ship ÷ total_epa_dha_mg_in_bottle) × 1000
```

| Marketing line | What to verify |
|---|---|
| “1000 mg fish oil” | EPA and DHA lines separately |
| “High strength” | Actual mg, serve count, softgels per day |
| Algae omega-3 | Still read EPA/DHA — useful for non-fish diets |

## Lifter context (no hype)

Omega-3 is **optional** relative to training, protein, and creatine. If oily fish already appears often in your diet, the capsule case is weaker. If diet is light on those foods, some people use a supplement as a convenience — still not a therapeutic promise.
