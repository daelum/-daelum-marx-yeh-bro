## TL;DR

Proprietary blend on a pre? **You’re guessing the dose.** Skip most of them.

- Buy **open labels** where citrulline, caffeine, beta-alanine (etc.) show **grams/mg**.
- A huge “Matrix 12,000 mg” number can still hide 1 g of the only ingredient you care about.
- AUD maths needs known doses — blends break the formula.
- Prefer labelled picks via [/go/pre-mn](/go/pre-mn) · [/go/pre-sm](/go/pre-sm).

## What a proprietary blend actually is

A branded bag of ingredients with **one total milligram weight** and **no per-ingredient amounts** (or amounts buried so you can’t audit the active you wanted).

Legal in many markets. Still a bad deal for evidence-first buyers.

## Why we skip most of them

| Problem | Straight talk |
|---|---|
| **Dose guessing** | You can’t know if citrulline is useful or dust |
| **AUD theatre** | Cheap tub + 2-scoop “full dose” = expensive sessions |
| **Stim surprises** | Caffeine can hide in a blend — bad for evening training / sleep |
| **Compare failure** | Can’t rank products on effective dose |
| **Hype cover** | Easy place for “explosive / natural steroid” copy — hard skip for Yeh Bro |

We are not saying every blended product is toxic. We’re saying **you’re shopping blind**.

## How to read a pre label in 30 seconds

1. Find **caffeine** — listed in mg? Good. Missing? Assume risk.
2. Find **citrulline / citrulline malate** — listed in **g**? Note the form.
3. Find **beta-alanine** — listed? Tingles ≠ quality score.
4. Spot **“Proprietary / Complex / Matrix”** with no breakdown → default **skip**.
5. Check **serves vs scoops needed** for the doses that *are* listed.

Illustrative AUD (verify live):

| Label style | Sticker | Serves on tub | Scoops for labelled actives | Effective AUD / session |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open formula | $55 | 25 | 1 | **~$2.20** |
| Blend + “40 serves” | $49 | 40 | 2 | **~$2.45**+ and still guessing |

## FAQ

**Are all blends scams?**  
No. They’re opaque. Opacity fails our buy filter.

**What if the brand “has clinical doses” in the ad but not on the panel?**  
Trust the panel. Ads lie easier than labels (still verify both).

**Can I use a blend if I only care about caffeine?**  
Then buy **labelled caffeine** or coffee — cheaper control. Don’t pay blend tax for a stim you can’t measure.

**Women?**  
Same label rules. Ignore gendered packaging.
