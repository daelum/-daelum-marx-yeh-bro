Flavour fights are entertainment. **Scoop maths** decides who wins. For label red flags and “protein per scoop vs powder weight,” see [protein per scoop](/blog/protein-per-scoop-australia) — this page is the **price maths**.

**TL;DR:** Divide tub price by (serves × protein grams per serve), then normalise to **$ per 25 g protein**. That’s the fair fight.

## The formula

```
$ / 25 g protein = (tub price AUD ÷ total protein grams in tub) × 25
```

Or: price ÷ serves ÷ protein-per-serve × 25.

Do it on the label in front of you. Promotions change weekly.

## What to read on the label

- Protein per serve (as sold, not fantasy “dry basis” games)
- Serve size in grams
- Serves per container
- Added sugars / junk if that matters for your cut

**Lactose wrecks you?** Isolate (WPI) can be worth the premium. That’s a gut call, not a detox — full comparison: [Whey concentrate vs isolate](/blog/whey-concentrate-vs-isolate-australia).

## Example framing (illustrative)

| Tub type | What usually wins |
|---|---|
| Big WPC bag on sale | Often best $/25 g |
| Premium WPI | Worth it for lactose or lean macros — not ego |
| “Mass gainer” | Cheap calories; not the same as protein economics |

We won’t invent live prices here — check the retailer the day you buy.
