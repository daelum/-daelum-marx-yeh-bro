- **Default dose:** **3–5 g creatine monohydrate per day**, every day.
- **Loading:** Optional. Speeds saturation; not required for results over a few weeks.

## The simple protocol (most lifters)

| Step | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Buy **creatine monohydrate** (micronised is fine) |
| 2 | Take **3–5 g once daily** |
| 3 | Mix in water, juice, or a shake — whatever you’ll actually drink |
| 4 | Keep going on rest days |
| 5 | Re-buy on AUD/serve maths, not flavour drops |

Missed a day? Resume tomorrow. No “double up punishment” required.

Not medical advice. If you have a relevant medical condition (e.g. kidney concerns), talk to a qualified professional before using creatine.

## Loading vs daily — maths without the myth

### Daily only (default)

- **3–5 g/day** from day one.
- Muscle creatine stores rise over roughly **3–4 weeks** for many people on this approach.
- Easier on the gut for some; simpler habit.

### Optional load

| Phase | Dose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Load | ~**20 g/day**, split into 4 × ~5 g | **5–7 days** |
| Maintain | **3–5 g/day** | Ongoing |

Loading gets you to saturation faster. It does **not** unlock a different kind of gain. If split doses upset your stomach, skip the load and stay on 3–5 g.

**Water note:** Early scale bumps are often water in the muscle, not fat.

## Timing myths vs what matters

| Myth | Straight answer |
|---|---|
| “Only post-workout or it doesn’t work” | False. Daily total matters more. |
| “Must take with grape juice / 100 g carbs” | Nice-to-have for some protocols; not a hard requirement for mono. |
| “Cycle 8 weeks on / 4 off” | No evidence-based need for most healthy lifters. |
| “Morning only / night only” | Pick a slot you’ll remember. |

**Practical AU tip:** Tie the scoop to a habit you already have — morning coffee, post-training shake, or dinner. Habit > optimisation.

## How to measure the scoop (honesty)

- Many tubs include a scoop near **3–5 g** — **weigh once** if you care. Scoop volume drifts with settling and brand.
- If the tub is a “creatine complex,” read **grams of creatine monohydrate**, not the full blend weight.
- Pre-workout scoops rarely replace a dedicated daily tub — see [stim-free pre](/blog/best-stim-free-pre-workout-australia).

## Body size, women, and “more is better”

- **Women:** Same **3–5 g** range for most. No pink special dose.
- **Larger athletes:** Some protocols use the upper end of common ranges; don’t invent megadoses because a forum said so.
- **More than ~5 g/day** for healthy recreational lifters is usually wasted powder, not faster progress.

## FAQ

**Can I mix creatine into hot coffee?**  
Yes for practical use. Taste stays chalky either way.

**Do I take it on rest days?**  
Yes. Saturation is a daily thing.

**What if I forget for a week?**  
Resume 3–5 g daily. Optional short load if you want to re-saturate faster — still optional.

**Is loading dangerous?**  
Split doses are the usual approach; gut comfort varies. This is not medical advice for your situation.
