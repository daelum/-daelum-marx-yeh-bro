**Scope:** Australia-first buying · seed `/go/` only.

## TL;DR

- **AU specialist shops** often win on category depth, brand relationships, and returns clarity for tubs.
- **Amazon AU** wins on convenience/speed *when the SKU is genuinely local-fulfilled* — still run label + AUD maths.
- Prefer **ships-from-AU** clarity; watch marketplace seller variance.
- Yeh Bro default gravity: brand/retailer seed paths first, Amazon as backup discovery.

Label literacy: [Supplement label red flags](/blog/supplement-label-red-flags-australia) · Hub: [Start here](/start-here)

## Side-by-side

| Factor | Amazon AU | AU supplement shops (e.g. SM / brand sites) |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Often strong with Prime-style delivery when stocked local | Varies by retailer |
| Selection | Broad; quality of listing varies by seller | Deeper specialty catalogues |
| Returns | Account-based; check seller | Often clearer for supplement-native retailers |
| Price honesty | Easy to miss shipping/seller fees | Still verify promo vs serve maths |
| Trust cues | Ratings help; counterfeit risk talk exists in categories | Brand-authorised vibe when buying direct |
| Best use | Backup / convenience SKU | Primary for creatine/protein/pre carts |

We don’t invent commission rates or “Amazon always cheaper” claims — prices move daily.

## Trust checklist (either channel)

1. Panel matches the front claim
2. Seller / stockist is clear (not a random overseas grey import)
3. AUD landed price includes shipping
4. Returns window known before you open the tub
5. No AAS/SARM/cycle marketing on the PDP — hard skip

## FAQ

**Is Amazon AU “fake tub” central?**  
Risk varies by category/seller. Buy fulfilled/reputable listings; when unsure, brand site or known AU retailer.

**Are specialist shops always dearer?**  
Not always after shipping and serve maths. Calculate.

**International Amazon?**  
AU-first audience: prefer AU fulfilment to avoid surprise freight/duties.
