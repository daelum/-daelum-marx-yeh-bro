**Scope:** Training troubleshooting · [Yeh Bro System](/start-here) · use with a [training logbook](/blog/training-logbook).

Lifts stall. That’s normal. What’s not normal is treating every flat week like a personality crisis and downloading a new program before you’ve checked sleep. This is the boring checklist I’d run before blaming genetics or buying another tub.

## TL;DR

- Confirm it’s a **real plateau** (weeks of flat like-for-like sets), not one bad Tuesday.
- Fix **sleep → food → stress/steps → deload → effort honesty** before program surgery.
- Program hops feel productive; they’re often avoidance — [ego lifting](/blog/ego-lifting-vs-training-age).
- Supplements don’t unstick a recovery problem. Creatine helps training quality; it isn’t a plateau spell — [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).

## Step 0 — Is it actually stuck?

Open the [logbook](/blog/training-logbook). For your main lifts:

- Same (or worse) load × reps at similar **RIR (reps in reserve)** for **3+ weeks**?
- Or did life explode and you missed half your sessions — [how many days](/blog/how-many-days-to-lift)?

Missed sessions aren’t a plateau. They’re a schedule problem.

## The checklist (in order)

### 1. Sleep

Protect a window most nights — [sleep for lifters](/blog/sleep-for-lifters). Four hours + max effort is cosplay. Late caffeine? [Caffeine dose](/blog/caffeine-dose-training).

### 2. Food (especially protein and fuel)

- Hitting a practical protein range? — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters)
- In a hard cut with no training carbs? — [carbs around training](/blog/carbs-around-training) · [cutting](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts)
- “Hunger noise” vs under-eating — [hunger on a cut](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut)

### 3. Stress and steps

Life stress taxes recovery like volume does — [stress and lifting](/blog/stress-and-lifting). Zero steps + desk day + hard lower isn’t “saving energy” — [walking](/blog/walking-for-lifters) · [rest days](/blog/rest-days-vs-active-recovery).

### 4. Deload before you reinvent the split

Drop volume ~30% for a week, keep some intensity if you like, sleep — [deload week](/blog/deload-week). Many “plateaus” were accumulated fatigue wearing a trench coat.

### 5. Effort honesty

Sandbagging at fake “3 RIR” forever stalls you. Daily absolute failure stalls you differently. Calibrate — [RPE / RIR](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve). Warm up enough that work sets aren’t surprise grinders — [warmup](/blog/warmup-before-lifting).

### 6. Then — and only then — program tweaks

| Tweak | When |
|---|---|
| Add a small set on a lagging pattern | Recovery is clearly fine |
| Swap a variation (e.g. incline vs flat) | Joint irritation, not boredom |
| Move full body → upper/lower | You’ve earned volume — [upper / lower](/blog/upper-lower-split) |
| New influencer split | Almost never week-one of a stall |

## What not to buy as a “fix”

- Thermogenic fairy dust
- Mystery pre that hides caffeine — [pre without hype](/blog/pre-workout-without-hype)
- Forum “blast through the plateau” pharmacology — literacy only, no sourcing — [PED hub](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia) · [forum lore vs clinical care](/blog/forum-cycle-lore-vs-clinical-care)

Legal basics that still make sense as *training nutrition*: daily creatine, protein convenience if food is awkward — [creatine for older lifters](/blog/creatine-for-older-lifters) if age is the lane · [Muscle Nation whey isolate](/go/protein-amz).
