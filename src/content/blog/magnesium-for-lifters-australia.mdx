## Compliance frame (read this)

Yeh Bro phase-1 voice: **food / general nutrition**, not therapeutic advertising.

| OK | Not OK |
|---|---|
| “Mineral often discussed for general nutrition” | “Cures insomnia / anxiety / depression” |
| “Check label for elemental Mg” | “Replaces medical care” |
| “Forms differ in cost and gut comfort for some” | “Detoxes muscles / lactic acid flush” |
| “Not medical advice” | Disease treatment or TGA-risky promises |

If you have a deficiency concern or medical condition, see a qualified professional — don’t self-prescribe from a blog.

## Forms (trade-offs, not miracles)

| Form (examples on labels) | Practical note |
|---|---|
| Oxide | Often cheap per bottle; elemental maths + gut comfort vary |
| Citrate / glycinate / other salts | Different cost and tolerance anecdotes — read **elemental mg** |
| Topicals | Outside our default oral scoop maths — don’t assume absorption stories |

We won’t rank a forever “best magnesium in Australia” without a dated criteria table. Prefer clear labels and boring expectations.

## How to compare tubs/bottles (AUD)

1. Find **elemental magnesium (mg) per serve** — not just compound weight.
2. Price in AUD including shipping.
3. Estimate **AUD per day** at the serve you actually take.
4. Prefer AU-friendly fulfilment when the gap is small.

## FAQ

**Will magnesium fix my sleep?**  
We won’t promise that. Sleep hygiene and total stress load matter; see a professional for ongoing sleep problems.

**Does it stop cramps?**  
Individual and context-dependent — not a guaranteed therapeutic claim here.

**Women?**  
Same label/elemental-mg rules. No special “hormone balance” Mg marketing from us.

**With creatine?**  
Generally discussed as separate habits; follow labels. Not medical advice.
