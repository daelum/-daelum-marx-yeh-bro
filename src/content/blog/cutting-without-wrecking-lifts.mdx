**Scope:** Fat loss while lifting · [Start here](/start-here) food step.

A cut should make you leaner, not weaker and miserable. If the bar is falling apart every week, the deficit (or the recovery) is wrong — not your “work ethic.”

## TL;DR

- Aim roughly **15–20% below maintenance** — adjust from the scale trend over 2–3 weeks.
- Keep **protein high** and training quality on the big lifts.
- Add **steps / easy walking**, not endless HIIT on empty.
- Expect some gym performance noise. Collapse is a signal to ease up or [deload](/blog/deload-week).

## Setup before you slash calories

1. You’ve been training consistently — see [full body](/blog/beginner-full-body-program) or your current split.
2. You roughly know maintenance (weight stable for a few weeks).
3. Sleep isn’t already a disaster — [sleep for lifters](/blog/sleep-for-lifters).

Cutting on top of chaos just teaches you that dieting “doesn’t work.”

## Deficit that doesn’t torch you

| Lever | Practical call |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~**15–20%** under maintenance to start |
| Rate | Roughly **0.5–1% body weight / week** for many — slower if already lean |
| Protein | Toward **~1.6–2.2+ g/kg** — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters) |
| Carbs | Bias around training so sessions don’t feel like sludge |
| Fats | Don’t zero them; keep a sane floor |

Track the **trend**, not one salty dinner. If weight isn’t moving for 2–3 weeks, adjust a little — don’t crash diet.

Powder for convenience on low appetite days: [/go/protein-true](/go/protein-true) · [whey vs food](/blog/whey-vs-food-protein).

Hunger *noise* (habit snacks, not true meal hunger) is a different problem — caffeine + a near-zero-cal ritual can help some lifters without eating the plan: [Energy drinks vs pre in a deficit](/blog/energy-drinks-vs-pre-workout-deficit).

## Training on a cut

- Keep **compound lifts**. Don’t switch to “toning” circuits.
- Progress when you can; **maintain** when you must — still logs [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) intent.
- Leave reps in reserve. Failure-every-set plus a deficit is a short story.
- Volume may need a trim if recovery tanks — that’s smarter than ego.

## Cardio that helps

- **Daily walking** is the cheat code for NEAT — [walking for lifters](/blog/walking-for-lifters).
- Optional: one harder conditioning session if sleep and lifts hold.
- Punishment cardio to “earn food” usually backfires.

## When strength drops

Some drop is normal as body weight falls. Panic when:

- Sleep and mood are wrecked
- Joints are angry
- Every lift is sliding for weeks

Then: slightly more food, fewer hard sets, or a [deload](/blog/deload-week). Stress management matters — [stress and lifting](/blog/stress-and-lifting).

Creatine can stay — water weight ≠ fat regain — [creatine myths](/blog/creatine-myths) · [dose](/blog/how-to-dose-creatine).

## Exit the cut on purpose

Don’t “surprise bulk” with takeaway month. Either reverse diet toward maintenance, or move into a planned small surplus — [lean bulk basics](/blog/lean-bulk-basics). Longer phased education: [blueprint phase 1 cut](/blog/bodybuilding-blueprint-phase-1-cut).
