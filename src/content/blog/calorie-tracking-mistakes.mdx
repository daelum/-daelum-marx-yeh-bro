Most “broken metabolisms” are **broken logs**. Under-reporting is common. So is over-precision on chicken breast while oils and snacks go freestyle. Fix the log before you cut another 500 kcal.

## TL;DR

- Weigh **oils, nut butters, cheese, granola** — the dense stuff.  
- Log **bites, tastes, drinks, weekends**.  
- Distrust generic restaurant database entries — [restaurants](/blog/hitting-macros-at-restaurants).  
- Use a short honest audit, then loosen — [tracking vs hand portions](/blog/tracking-vs-hand-portions).

## High-frequency errors

| Mistake | Why it wrecks the math |
|---|---|
| “Cooking spray / glug of oil” unlogged | Fat is ~9 kcal/g — teaspoons add up |
| Package “per serve” vs what you poured | Cereal, pasta, peanut butter classic |
| Weekend amnesia | Two untracked dinners erase five clean weekdays |
| Restaurant menu button cosplay | Databases guess; kitchens pour |
| Protein powder heaped scoops | — [label math](/blog/label-math-drill) · [protein per scoop](/blog/protein-per-scoop-australia) |
| “Training hard so I earned mystery snacks” | Exercise calories are easy to overestimate |
| Only logging perfect days | Average intake is what the body sees |

## Over-reporting traps (less common, still real)

- Counting food you didn’t finish  
- Double-logging a shake and the powder  
- Inflating burns from watches, then eating them back every session

**NEAT** (non-exercise activity) and watch “calories burned” are noisy — prefer intake honesty + weekly weight trend — [estimate maintenance](/blog/estimate-maintenance-calories) · [step targets](/blog/step-targets-without-obsessing-neat).

## Audit protocol (7–14 days)

1. Weigh calorie-dense foods.  
2. Log everything you swallow — including alcohol — [alcohol tax](/blog/alcohol-training-quiet-tax).  
3. Keep steps roughly normal.  
4. Compare average intake to weight trend.  
5. Adjust phase calories — [recalculating](/blog/recalculating-calories-after-weight-change).
