Finishing a cut feels like a jailbreak. That’s when people undo months of patience in two weeks of “I earned this.” Transition mistakes aren’t moral failures — they’re predictable. Here’s the list and the fix.

## TL;DR

- Don’t smash from deficit → unlimited surplus overnight.  
- Keep **protein** high while calories climb — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters).  
- Use [reverse diet](/blog/reverse-diet-literacy) or a controlled lean surplus — not a dirty bulk.  
- Watch weekly averages — [weight / waist / photos](/blog/tracking-weight-waist-photos).

## Mistake table

| Mistake | What happens | Better move |
|---|---|---|
| Unlimited takeaway week | Fast fat regain; scale panic | Planned bumps; keep structure — [meal structure](/blog/meal-structure-without-apps) |
| Cutting protein “because bulk” | Worse satiety + soft recomp | Hold lifter protein range |
| Zero steps after walking daily | Expenditure crash | Keep [step targets](/blog/step-targets-without-obsessing-neat) sane |
| Max training volume day one of surplus | Joints + recovery blow up | Resume progressive plan — [powerbuilding](/blog/powerbuilding-hybrid-week) later |
| Dropping creatine for “water weight optics” | Pointless | Keep [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz) |
| Comparing to enhanced bulk timelines | Bad expectations | [Natty or not](/blog/natty-or-not-physique-standards) · [influencer literacy](/blog/enhanced-influencer-media-literacy) |

## Hunger after the cut

Appetite can roar when you exit a deficit. That’s physiology + habit. Use the same noise-vs-need skills — [hunger on a cut](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut) — and place carbs around training — [carbs around training](/blog/carbs-around-training). Refeeds were tools *during* the cut; they aren’t a personality after — [refeeds](/blog/refeeds-and-diet-breaks).
