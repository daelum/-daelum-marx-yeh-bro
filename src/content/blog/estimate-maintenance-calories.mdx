**Maintenance** is the intake where your weekly weight trend is basically flat. Apps spit a number from **BMR**/**RMR** (basal/resting metabolic rate) × activity. Treat that as a **guess**, then verify with the scale. You’re not marrying MyFitnessPal.

## TL;DR

- **TDEE** (total daily energy expenditure) ≈ what you burn in a day on average. Maintenance ≈ TDEE.  
- Start with a rough formula **or** a two-week eating audit. Confirm with weight trend.  
- Cut: ~**10–20%** below. Bulk: ~**5–15%** above. Recheck after weight changes — [recalculating](/blog/recalculating-calories-after-weight-change).  
- Fix reporting lies before you blame “metabolism” — [tracking mistakes](/blog/calorie-tracking-mistakes).

## Glossary (once)

| Acronym | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BMR** / **RMR** | Energy at rest (estimates from age/sex/weight/height) |
| **TDEE** | Whole-day burn: rest + food processing (**TEF**, thermic effect of food) + **NEAT** (non-exercise activity) + exercise |
| **NEAT** | Steps, fidgeting, standing — often the silent variable — [step targets](/blog/step-targets-without-obsessing-neat) |

## Method A — quick multiplier (good enough)

1. Estimate **BMR** with any reputable calculator (Harris–Benedict / Mifflin — pick one and stop shopping formulas).  
2. Multiply by activity:

| Lifestyle | Rough multiplier |
|---|---|
| Desk + lifts 3×/week | ~1.4–1.55 |
| On feet a lot + lifts | ~1.55–1.7 |
| Very active job / high NEAT | ~1.7–1.9 |

That’s your **starting TDEE guess**. Not gospel.

## Method B — two-week audit (better)

1. Eat how you normally eat. Track roughly (app, notes, or weighed staples).  
2. Weigh daily, average the week.  
3. If weight is flat → your average intake ≈ maintenance.  
4. Rising ~0.25–0.5%/week → you’re in a surplus; falling → deficit. Back-calculate.

Hand portions instead of an app? Fine for the audit if portions are consistent — [tracking vs hand portions](/blog/tracking-vs-hand-portions) · [meal structure](/blog/meal-structure-without-apps).
