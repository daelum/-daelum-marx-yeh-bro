You have three tools: a **food scale**, an **app**, and **hand portions**. None is morality. Pick the mode that matches the phase and your brain.

## TL;DR

- **Scale + short audit** when you’re guessing wild or stalling — [tracking mistakes](/blog/calorie-tracking-mistakes).  
- **Hand portions** for long-term adherence — [meal structure](/blog/meal-structure-without-apps).  
- **Apps** as teachers, not lifelong ankle monitors.  
- Restaurants need a different playbook — [hitting macros at restaurants](/blog/hitting-macros-at-restaurants).

## Mode comparison

| Mode | Best for | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| Food scale | Learning true portions; tight cuts | Anxiety / avoiding social food |
| App logging | 1–4 week education; plateaus | Decimal-place procrastination |
| Hand portions | Default life; travel; maintenance | Drift if palms never recalibrate |

## A sane sequence

1. **2 weeks** weighed staples (rice, oats, meat, oil) so your eyes calibrate.  
2. Switch to **hand portions** + repeat meals.  
3. Re-weigh for a week if the scale trend dies — [why am I not losing?](/blog/why-am-i-not-losing-fat).  
4. Recalculate targets after meaningful weight change — [recalculating calories](/blog/recalculating-calories-after-weight-change).

Flexible dieting and meal plans both work inside these modes — [flexible vs meal plans](/blog/flexible-dieting-vs-meal-plans).
