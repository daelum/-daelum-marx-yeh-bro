## TL;DR

- **Stim-free** means no (or negligible) caffeine — useful for evening sessions, caffeine-sensitive lifters, or when you already drank coffee.
- **Buy labelled doses.** Proprietary blend on a pre? You’re guessing. Skip it.
- **Useful levers (when dosed properly):** citrulline (check the label), beta-alanine (tingles ≠ “working harder”), creatine belongs in a **daily** tub more than a pre scoop.
- **Coffee is valid.** Sometimes the honest AUD winner is caffeine you already understand.

We will not pretend a legal pre is “like steroids but natural.”

## What matters on the label

### 1. Full disclosure of doses

If the panel says “Pump Matrix 12,000 mg” with six ingredients and no breakdown, you cannot know if citrulline is 1 g or 8 g. **Skip proprietary blends.**

### 2. Ingredients with a real job

| Ingredient | What to look for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **L-Citrulline or citrulline malate** | Dose listed in grams | Malate vs pure — don’t assume 1:1 |
| **Beta-alanine** | Often ~3+ g/day cumulative for tingles | Tingles ≠ proof of a good formula |
| **Creatine** | Nice if present; **daily mono tub** is cleaner economics | Don’t rely on pre scoops for 3–5 g/day |
| **Electrolytes** | Sodium/potassium listed | Handy in hot AU gyms; not magic |
| **Nootropic pile** | Caution | Easy to overclaim; keep expectations boring |

Avoid disease, hormone, “PCT,” and fat-burner medical claims.

### 3. AUD per effective scoop

A tub with 40 “serves” that need a 2-scoop serving for labelled amounts is a 20-serve tub in practice.

## Stim-free vs coffee vs stim pre

| Option | Control | Cost | Sleep risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coffee / caffeine tablet** | High (you pick mg) | Usually cheap | Depends on timing/dose |
| **Stim pre** | Medium (fixed formula) | Medium–high | Higher late day |
| **Stim-free pre** | Medium (formula) | Medium | Lower from caffeine |

If your only goal is alertness, coffee often wins. If you want citrulline/beta-alanine without caffeine, stim-free has a lane.

## Red flags (skip list)

- Proprietary blends hiding the only ingredients that matter
- “Explosive gains / steroid-like pump” copy
- Underdosed citrulline behind a big proprietary number
- Grey imports with sketchy AU customs risk
- Anything pitching AAS, SARMs, or cycle support — **hard ban**

## AU buying notes

- Prefer formulas stocked by **AU retailers**
- “Stim-free” should mean **no caffeine**, not “natural energy mystery”
- Don’t pay a 40% AUD premium for a collab tub with the same panel
- Reformulations happen — date-stamp your decision

## Who should buy / who should skip

**Buy stim-free if:** you train late, caffeine messes you up, or you want citrulline/beta-alanine without another stim hit.

**Skip if:** the label won’t show doses, you’re a beginner who hasn’t sorted protein + creatine, or you’re buying hype.

**DIY:** citrulline tub + electrolytes + your own caffeine timing is a valid path.

## FAQ

**Will stim-free still give me tingles?**  
If it has enough beta-alanine, yes. Tingles are paresthesia — not a quality score.

**Can I take stim-free every session?**  
Follow the label. Watch total daily beta-alanine and other actives. Not medical advice.

**Is nitric oxide marketing real?**  
Many products lean on citrulline-related pathways and pump feel. Expect training support language — not pharmaceutical promises.
