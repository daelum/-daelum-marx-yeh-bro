## Read this with the phases

Training and food in the blueprint are meant to be usable. Anything about AAS amounts is **culture literacy** — common patterns people discuss — **not** Yeh Bro medical advice. Many AAS are prescription-only / controlled. We do not sell or source gear. See a doctor.

## Blood pressure

In Phase 3 especially, culture chatter + higher calories can push BP up fast.

Culture often cites: if home readings sit **consistently above ~140/90 mmHg**, people talk about lowering pharmacological exposure and fixing lifestyle stressors. Yeh Bro’s education line: **treat sustained high BP as a medical issue** — contact a clinician; do not wait for a forum vote. Your clinician sets your personal thresholds.

## Fatigue vs systemic strain

| Expected | Warning pattern |
|---|---|
| Local muscular soreness | Persistent joint pain that does not settle |
| Hard sessions | Resting heart rate **~10+ bpm** over your normal baseline for days |
| Being tired after a cut | Systemic lethargy despite high calories and sleep |

Education response many coaches use: **deload** — drop training volume ~30% for a week while keeping food (and, if relevant, whatever clinical plan you already have) steady. That is recovery craft, not a pharmacology tip.

## Aromatisation, sides, and AI misuse

Why cut-first literacy exists: higher body fat + androgens → more aromatisation talk (water, gynaecomastia risk).

AI (aromatase inhibitor) folklore is high-risk content:

- “Crush E2 preventatively” is a common misuse pattern  
- Low oestrogen can feel awful; lipids and joints often enter the conversation  
- These are prescription medicines in clinical contexts — **not** Yeh Bro affiliate products  

If oestrogenic or androgenic sides appear: **medical care**, not a blind AI protocol from a fitness site.

## Mental health & crisis

Mood, body image, and dependency on the “next run” are part of PED culture cost. Supports: GP, psychologist, [Lifeline](https://www.lifeline.org.au/) (13 11 14), [ADF](https://adf.org.au/). Coming off language = stopping and recovering under care — not a PCT affiliate pack ([PED guide](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia)).

**Urgent:** chest pain, suicidal ideation, severe mood change, jaundice, acute illness → emergency / urgent care.
