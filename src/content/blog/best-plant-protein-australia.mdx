**Scope:** Legal sports nutrition · seed `/go/` only.

## TL;DR

- Plant protein is a **fair call** if you avoid dairy or prefer it — not a detox or automatic “cleaner” whey killer.
- Check **protein per scoop**, blend completeness, and **AUD per 25 g protein** after shipping.
- Single-source pea/rice can work in a whole-diet context; many lifters prefer **blends** for convenience.
- No dedicated plant seed slug yet — browse via [/go/protein-mypro](/go/protein-mypro) · [/go/protein-sm](/go/protein-sm). Do **not** invent brand slugs until Ops promotes them to seed.

## Checklist before you buy

| Check | Pass looks like |
|---|---|
| Protein / serve (g) | Clear panel number — not just front claim |
| Scoop maths | AUD / 25 g after shipping — [Cheapest protein](/blog/cheapest-protein-powder-australia) |
| Ingredient list | Pea / rice / hemp / soy etc. listed; no mystery “plant matrix” with zero grams |
| Sweetener / flavour | You’ll actually drink it (waste = expensive) |
| AU stock / returns | Local retailer or known AU shipper |
| Claims | Food/sports nutrition only — skip hormone/detox theatre |

Scoop literacy: [Protein per scoop](/blog/protein-per-scoop-australia).

## Amino profile — practical, not mystical

| Approach | Yeh Bro take |
|---|---|
| Blended plant proteins | Often the convenient way to round out amino pattern in one scoop |
| Single-source + varied diet | Can work if daily food already diversifies protein sources |
| “Complete = steroid gains” marketing | Skip — powder still fills gaps around training/food |

We don’t crown eternal “best plant in Australia” without date + criteria. Method lives on the protein pillar.

## Plant vs whey (honest)

| | Plant | Whey |
|---|---|---|
| Dairy-free | Yes (check label for cross-contact if sensitive) | No |
| Typical AUD / 25 g | Often higher | WPC often wins |
| Lactose | N/A | Isolate if needed |
| Beginner default | When dairy is a hard no | Usually whey first |
