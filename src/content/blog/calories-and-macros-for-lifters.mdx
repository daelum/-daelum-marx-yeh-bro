**Scope:** Food rabbit-hole hub · pairs with [Yeh Bro System · food](/start-here#step-3). Read this, then dive.

If you lift and you’re confused why the scale won’t move, it’s usually **calories in vs calories out** over weeks — not a missing magic macro ratio. **Macros** (macronutrients: protein, carbohydrate, fat) are how you split those calories. This hub wires the whole cluster so you can stay for hours without app worship.

## TL;DR

- **Calories** = energy. Surplus → often gain; deficit → often lose fat (with noise).  
- Find rough **maintenance**, then nudge for cut/bulk.  
- Protein first, then carbs for training, fat for the floor — not Instagram pie charts.  
- Tracking is a tool; hand portions work too. Fix reporting mistakes before buying another tub.
