**Scope:** Australia-first · seed `/go/` only.

## TL;DR

- Most lifters on **60–90 min** sessions do fine with **water + a normal diet**. Intra is optional.
- **Electrolytes** can make sense in **hot AU gyms / long sweaty sessions** — not as a detox or cramp miracle.
- **Intra carbs** are more relevant for longer/harder volume than a short pump session.
- No dedicated electrolyte seed slug — browse labelled options via [/go/pre-sm](/go/pre-sm); don’t invent SKUs.

Core stack first: [Beginner stack](/blog/beginner-supplement-stack-australia) · [/go/stack-beginner-sm](/go/stack-beginner-sm)

## Who might benefit

| Situation | Electrolytes | Intra carbs |
|---|---|---|
| Short lift, cool room, eat normally | Usually optional | Usually optional |
| Long session, heavy sweat, hot warehouse gym | More plausible | Maybe if energy drops |
| Endurance block / high volume day | Context-dependent | More plausible |
| Beginner still missing protein/creatine | **Not priority** | **Not priority** |

We won’t claim electrolytes “cure cramps” or treat medical conditions. Persistent cramping/dizziness → professional care, not another powder.

## Label honesty

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Sodium / potassium listed | Know what you’re buying |
| Sugar / carb grams per serve | Intra carb vs flavoured water |
| Serve size vs “20 serves” vanity | Same scoop-tax games as pre |
| Proprietary “Hydration Matrix” | Often a skip — [Proprietary blend pre-workout](/blog/proprietary-blend-pre-workout) |

Pre vs coffee for stims: [Pre-workout vs coffee](/blog/pre-workout-vs-coffee). Don’t stack mystery stim + mystery intra blindly.

## DIY vs tub

| Path | When it fits |
|---|---|
| Water + food | Default |
| Pinch of salt in water / salty meal | Simple sweat replacement talk — not medical dosing advice |
| Labelled electrolyte product | Convenience in heat |
| Gamer-looking intra with 30 ingredients | Usually marketing |

AUD: a fancy tub can lose to food + water fast. Same mindset as [Cheapest protein](/blog/cheapest-protein-powder-australia).

## FAQ

**Do I need intra on a cut?**  
Only if performance tanks and food timing is rough — not because TikTok said so.

**Women?**  
Same heat/sweat logic. No special pink hydration required.

**With magnesium?**  
Different products/jobs — [Magnesium for lifters](/blog/magnesium-for-lifters-australia). Don’t stack five “recovery” stories.
