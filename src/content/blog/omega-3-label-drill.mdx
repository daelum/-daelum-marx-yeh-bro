Omega-3 marketing loves “heart health” fog. Lifters just need honest **EPA** (eicosapentaenoic acid) and **DHA** (docosahexaenoic acid) maths — then decide if a bottle is even worth it. This is a drill, not a disease-treatment claim sheet.

**Not medical advice** — bleeding risk, meds, and surgery timing are clinician topics.

## TL;DR

- Ignore “fish oil 1000 mg” on the front — read **EPA + DHA mg per softgel** on the panel.  
- Compare **cost per gram of EPA+DHA**, not bottle size flex.  
- Optional basic — food (oily fish) first; capsules fill gaps.  
- Soft live hop: [Blackmores Omega Triple](/go/omega-amz) · deeper guide: [omega-3 for lifters](/blog/omega-3-for-lifters-australia).

## Red flags

- Front-of-bottle “1000 mg fish oil” with tiny EPA/DHA on the back  
- Detox / miracle curing language  
- Rancid smell when opened  
- Stacking seventeen anti-inflammatory powders instead of sleep and training recovery
