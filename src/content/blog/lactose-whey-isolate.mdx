## Lactose vs “this powder hates me”

| Clue | More like lactose / dairy tolerance | More like something else |
|---|---|---|
| Bloat/urgency after concentrate shakes | Common pattern | — |
| Fine with hard cheese / yoghurt but not whey | Possible; still individual | — |
| Only happens when creatine is *in* the shake | Test creatine alone in water — [Does creatine cause bloating?](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating) | Don’t blame mono by default |
| Rash, severe symptoms | Stop; see a professional | Not a DIY blog diagnose |

Not medical advice. Suspected allergy or clinical intolerance → qualified care, not another tub experiment.

## Decision tree (keep it short)

1. **Tolerate dairy?** → Start value **WPC** (usually best AUD / 25 g) — [Cheapest protein](/blog/cheapest-protein-powder-australia)
2. **Concentrate wrecks you?** → Try **isolate** (typically less lactose) via a clear WPI on the panel.
3. **Still no / avoid dairy?** → Plant blend; don’t pretend it’s automatically cheaper.
4. **Either way:** run AUD / 25 g after shipping.

## FAQ

**Is isolate lactose-free?**  
Usually **lower** lactose than concentrate — not a medical guarantee. Read the label; your response matters.

**Can I use lactase drops instead?**  
Some people do with food — still not personal medical advice. Isolate is the simpler powder swap for many.

**Plant complete enough?**  
Blend for amino coverage; verify protein/scoop.

**Women?**  
Same lactose/maths rules. No pink-tax requirement.
