**Macros** = macronutrients: **protein**, **carbohydrate**, **fat**. They each provide calories (roughly 4 / 4 / 9 kcal per gram). For lifters, the useful order is: set calories for the phase → lock protein → set a fat floor → fill the rest with carbs (and a bit of fat for food you enjoy).

## TL;DR

- Calories set the phase; macros set the *feel* and training support.  
- Protein: ~**1.6–2.2 g/kg** — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters).  
- Fat: don’t crash the floor — [dietary fat floors](/blog/dietary-fat-floors-for-lifters).  
- Carbs: mostly leftovers for performance — [carb totals vs timing](/blog/carb-totals-vs-timing-for-lifters).

## A boring split that works

1. **Pick calories** — maintenance, cut, or bulk — [estimate maintenance](/blog/estimate-maintenance-calories).  
2. **Protein grams** → multiply × 4 for protein calories.  
3. **Fat floor** — often ~**0.6–1.0 g/kg** as a practical band (individual; not medical dosing).  
4. **Remaining calories → carbs** (and optional extra fat if you prefer fatty foods).

Example shape (illustrative, not a prescription): 80 kg lifter, 2,600 kcal cut-ish day → protein 160 g → fat 70 g → carbs fill the rest. Adjust to hunger and gym performance.

## What not to obsess over

| Trap | Reality |
|---|---|
| Exact 40/40/20 forever | Arbitrary marketing |
| “No carbs after 6” | Timing ≪ totals for fat loss — [carb totals vs timing](/blog/carb-totals-vs-timing-for-lifters) |
| Zero-fat challenge | Hurts adherence and often food quality — [fat floors](/blog/dietary-fat-floors-for-lifters) |
| Protein theatre ±5 g | Hit the range most days — [protein timing vs total](/blog/protein-timing-vs-total-intake) |
