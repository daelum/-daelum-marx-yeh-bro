## Why people cut before “building”

In enhanced gym culture, starting a high-androgen phase at a high body-fat percentage is widely treated as a mistake — not because fat is moral failure, but because **aromatisation risk rises**: more conversion of androgens toward oestrogenic pathways, with side-effect chatter around water retention and gynaecomastia.

Natural lifters still benefit from the same training logic: get reasonably lean, then surplus. The chemistry talk is **context for culture literacy**, not a cue to self-medicate.

## What is actionable vs what is culture talk

| Actionable (training / nutrition) | Culture literacy only (not site medical advice) |
|---|---|
| Energy deficit / maintenance / modest surplus maths | Specific AAS compounds and mg/week ranges people discuss |
| Protein, carb timing around training, fat floors | Injection split patterns discussed on forums |
| Upper/Lower → Push/Pull/Legs progression | Aromatase-inhibitor “keep on hand” folklore — misuse risks |
| Strength retention in a cut; double progression in a bulk | Escalating dose after a cut without clinical care |
