## TL;DR verdict

- **Most lifters:** **Creatine monohydrate.** Best evidence base and usually the best **AUD per effective dose**.
- **HCL:** Fine if you personally prefer the mix/gut feel **and** you accept paying more per gram of creatine — not because it’s “next gen steroids for natural athletes” (hard ban on that framing).
- **Neither:** Skip mystery “creatine matrices” that hide the grams.

## What matters (criteria)

1. **Dose equivalence** — how much creatine you’re actually getting
2. **AUD per effective dose** (normalise to ~3–5 g creatine/day intent)
3. **Mixability / gut comfort** (subjective; don’t overclaim)

## Monohydrate in one minute

- Most studied form.
- Typical daily target: **3–5 g**.
- Micronised versions mix better than old chalk bricks.
- Creapure = branded mono supply chain — pay extra only if you want that story.

## HCL in one minute

- Creatine hydrochloride — different salt, often marketed for solubility and smaller scoops.
- Marketing frequently implies “better absorption = smaller dose = better value.” **Run the AUD maths anyway.**
- Evidence volume vs monohydrate is not in the same league for most consumers’ decision — mono remains the Yeh Bro default.

## FAQ

**Is HCL better for avoiding bloat?**  
Bloat talk is usually water in muscle with creatine use generally. Don’t pick HCL solely on a guaranteed anti-bloat promise.

**Can I switch from HCL to mono?**  
Yes. Use the standard 3–5 g mono daily approach. Optional load if you want — [How to dose creatine](/blog/how-to-dose-creatine).

**Is ethyl ester / buffered creatine the real winner?**  
Usually not on value or need. Mono first.
