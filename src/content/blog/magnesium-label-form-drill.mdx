Magnesium bottles love “calm” and “sleep” vibes. Lifters need **elemental magnesium mg**, form honesty, and whether the bottle is even worth it. Optional basic — not a hypnotic prescription.

**Not medical advice** — kidney issues and meds change the story; ask a clinician.

## TL;DR

- Read **elemental Mg per tablet**, not just compound weight.  
- Forms differ in tolerance (glycinate often gentler than oxide for many people) — see [mag guide](/blog/magnesium-for-lifters-australia).  
- Soft live hop: [Nature’s Own magnesium glycinate](/go/mag-amz).  
- Sleep still needs darkness and timing — [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters) · [sleep trackers](/blog/sleep-trackers-useful-or-anxiety).
