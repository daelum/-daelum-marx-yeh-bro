Magnesium bottles love “calm” and “sleep” vibes. Lifters need **elemental magnesium mg**, form honesty, and whether the bottle is even worth it. Optional basic — not a hypnotic prescription, not a recovery personality.

**Not medical advice** — kidney issues, certain medications, and GI problems change the story; ask a clinician.

## TL;DR

- Read **elemental Mg per tablet/serve**, not just compound weight (glycinate salt grams ≠ elemental magnesium grams).
- Forms differ in tolerance (glycinate often gentler than oxide for many people) — see [mag guide](/blog/magnesium-for-lifters-australia).
- Soft live hop: [Nature’s Own magnesium glycinate](/go/mag-amz).
- Sleep still needs darkness and timing — [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters) · [sleep trackers](/blog/sleep-trackers-useful-or-anxiety).
- Mag sits below train / sleep / protein / creatine in stack priority for most beginners.

## Why elemental mg matters

Supplement labels sometimes lead with the weight of the **compound** (e.g. magnesium glycinate or oxide). What you’re actually shopping for is **elemental magnesium** — the Mg portion. Two bottles can look similar on the front and differ wildly once you find elemental mg per serve.

**Opinion:** if a brand won’t make elemental mg easy to find, treat that as a soft red flag and compare a clearer bottle. **Established shopping habit:** same drill as protein — price the thing you care about (here: elemental mg), not the tub story.

## Form notes (practical, not a chemistry exam)

| Form (common) | Practical note |
|---|---|
| Glycinate | Often chosen for gentler GI; popular “evening” pick |
| Citrate | Common; some people get looser stools at higher serves |
| Oxide | Cheap per label gram; elemental yield and tolerance often disappointing for many users |
| Other chelates / blends | Fine if elemental mg is clear; ignore “superior absorption” without readable doses |

Exact absorption debates online run forever. For buying: **clear elemental mg + personal tolerance + price per day** beats brand mythology.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing compound weight across forms as if it were elemental magnesium
- Buying “sleep magnesium” marketing instead of reading the panel
- Expecting mag to fix late caffeine and phone-in-bed habits
- Stacking mag before creatine and protein on a beginner budget
- Ignoring clinician advice when you have kidney disease or relevant meds
