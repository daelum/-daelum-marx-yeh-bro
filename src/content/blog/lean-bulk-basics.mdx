**Scope:** Muscle-gain nutrition · [Start here](/start-here).

A lean bulk is not a personality based on pizza. It’s a **small surplus** with training that actually progresses — then you stay patient longer than the algorithm wants.

## TL;DR

- Add roughly **~10% above maintenance** (or enough to gain slowly).
- Keep **protein** in a lifter range — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters).
- Push [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) on a schedule you can recover from.
- If the waist races ahead of the logbook, the surplus is too loud.

## Who should bulk

Bulk when:

- You’re training consistently (often 3–4 days — [frequency guide](/blog/how-many-days-to-lift))
- Sleep isn’t trashed
- You’ve finished a cut **or** you’re already at a body-fat level you’re happy to grow from

If you’re new and under-muscled at a higher body fat, a long **recomp** (maintenance calories + hard training) can be smarter than a dirty surplus. Honesty beats ego.

## Surplus without the dirty bulk

| Lever | Practical call |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~**+250–400 kcal/day** for many, or ~**10%** over maintenance |
| Gain rate | Slow — think modest monthly scale change, not a holiday |
| Protein | **~1.6–2.2 g/kg** |
| Carbs | Fuel the lifts; this is where most surplus often goes |
| Fats | Enough for hormones/food enjoyment — not the only surplus lever |

Adjust from the **trend**: strength up, measurements/photos, waist. Scale alone lies with water, creatine, and salt — [creatine myths](/blog/creatine-myths).

## Training is the point

Food without progressive overload is just expensive soft tissue.

- Keep a simple split — [full body](/blog/beginner-full-body-program) or Upper/Lower
- Add load or reps over weeks
- Deload when needed — [deload week](/blog/deload-week)
- Walk so NEAT doesn’t collapse into pure sedentary surplus — [walking](/blog/walking-for-lifters)

## Appetite and convenience

Some people struggle to eat enough. Then:

- Add calorie-dense sides (rice, oils, dairy, nut butters) instead of force-feeding junk only
- Use a shake to fill protein/calorie gaps — [whey vs food](/blog/whey-vs-food-protein) · [/go/protein-true](/go/protein-true)
- Creatine remains the boring daily scoop — [/go/creatine-true](/go/creatine-true)

## When to stop the bulk

- Waist and body fat climbing faster than strength
- Sleep apnea / health markers worrying you — see a clinician
- You care more about a look for a date than another training block — pivot to maintenance or a controlled cut ([cutting guide](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts))

Phased culture education (later, optional): [blueprint lean bulk](/blog/bodybuilding-blueprint-phase-3-lean-bulk). That’s literacy — not a cue to escalate pharmacology.
