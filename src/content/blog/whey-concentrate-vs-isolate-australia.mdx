**Scope:** Australia-first (AUD scoop maths, AU tubs, shipping).

## TL;DR

- **Concentrate (WPC):** Usually cheaper per 25 g protein. Fine if dairy sits well.
- **Isolate (WPI):** More protein per scoop, less lactose/fat — worth it if concentrate wrecks your gut or you want leaner macros.
- **Neither is a detox.** Isolate is not “cleaner magic.” It’s a filtration/processing difference.
- **Buy on maths:** AUD per **25 g protein**, not flavour hype or “best in the world” claims.

## What actually differs

| Factor | Whey concentrate (WPC) | Whey isolate (WPI) |
|---|---|---|
| Typical protein % | ~70–80% of powder weight | ~90%+ of powder weight |
| Lactose / fat | Higher | Lower |
| Price (AUD) | Usually lower | Usually higher |
| Use case | Default bulk protein | Lactose-sensitive / tighter macros |

Both are milk-derived proteins with solid leucine content for lifters when total daily protein is on target. The powder does not replace training or food — it fills gaps.

## Scoop maths (the only comparison that matters)

1. Read **protein per serve (g)** on the panel — not just scoop size in grams of powder.
2. Note **serves per container** and **AUD price** (include shipping if not free to your postcode).
3. Compute **AUD per 25 g protein** = `(price ÷ total protein grams in tub) × 25`

## Lactose and gut: when isolate is the fair call

If concentrate gives you bloating, urgency, or “I can’t train after this shake,” try:

1. **Smaller serves** mixed into food (oats, yoghurt) — sometimes enough.
2. **Isolate** — typically lower lactose.
3. **Plant blend** — if dairy is a hard no (no phase-1 plant `/go/` — request Affiliate Ops before linking).

Isolate is a **tolerance and macro tool**, not a gut cleanse. Skip any brand claiming to “detox your gut and shred fat with this powder.”

## Label red flags

- Protein claim that doesn’t match the nutrition panel maths
- Proprietary blends that bury actual protein source order
- “Anabolic / hormone / PCT” cosplay on a whey tub — hard skip
- Serving size games (tiny scoop = impressive “protein %” optics)
- No AU stock or absurd shipping without disclosure

## Concentrate vs isolate vs “hydrolysed”

Hydrolysed whey is often pricier, sometimes easier on the stomach for some people, not automatically “more gains.” Phase-1 default is **WPC vs WPI** unless a hydrolysed SKU earns its AUD gap.

## FAQ

**Is isolate better for building muscle?**  
Not meaningfully if total daily protein and training match. Isolate’s edge is composition and tolerance, not magic hypertrophy.

**Can I use whey to replace meals forever?**  
Convenient protein source — whole-food diet quality still matters. Not meal-replacement medical advice.

**How much protein per day?**  
Common sports-nutrition ranges for lifters often land roughly around **1.6–2.2 g/kg** body mass depending on goals — general info, not a prescription.

**Women and whey?**  
Same protein maths. Ignore “for her” pink tax unless the product actually fits macros/budget better.
