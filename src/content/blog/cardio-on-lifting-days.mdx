**Scope:** Conditioning placement · [walking for lifters](/blog/walking-for-lifters) · [Start here · training](/start-here#step-2).

Cardio doesn’t “kill gains” by existing. Poorly timed hard conditioning on top of heavy lower days does. Default: **walk most days**; add harder cardio where recovery allows.

## TL;DR

- Easy steps are almost always fine — [step targets](/blog/step-targets-without-obsessing-neat).  
- Hard HIIT after heavy squats is how weekends get weird — [weekend warrior](/blog/weekend-warrior-damage-control).  
- Prefer cardio **after** lifting or on separate days when possible.  
- Cuts: walking > punishment finishers — [cutting](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts).

## Placement table

| Goal | Better placement |
|---|---|
| General health / NEAT | Walk daily, including lift days |
| Fat loss support | Steps steady; optional easy bike after lift |
| Conditioning skill | Separate day or after upper sessions |
| Powerbuilding focus | Protect lower-day recovery — [powerbuilding](/blog/powerbuilding-hybrid-week) |

Warm up for lifts first — [warmup circuit](/blog/warmup-circuit-you-keep) — don’t replace ramp sets with a random 20-minute metcon.

## Interference without the dogma

- Hard cardio + hard hinges same day → watch RIR and next-day bar speed — [RPE](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve) · [hinges](/blog/joint-friendly-hinge-progressions).  
- Sleep debt + double hard sessions → deload territory — [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style) · [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters).  
- Fuel long days — [intra carbs](/blog/intra-carbs-for-long-sessions) · [carbs around training](/blog/carbs-around-training).

Creatine still helps high-intensity work — [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).

## Soft close

Walk like it’s free recovery. Conditionally condition. Keep the logbook climbing — [Start here](/start-here).
