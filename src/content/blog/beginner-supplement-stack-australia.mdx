**Scope:** Starter stack · phase-1 legal sports nutrition only.

## TL;DR

AU beginner stack under a sane budget:

1. **Creatine monohydrate** — 3–5 g/day
2. **Protein powder** — only if food alone misses your target (WPC default; WPI if lactose wrecks you)
3. **Caffeine** — coffee or a labelled dose; fancy pre is optional

Everything else can wait. No 12-tub “advanced” carts. No “on-cycle stack” shop framing.

Hub + checklist signup: [/start-here](/start-here)

## Order of operations (before you buy powder)

| Priority | Do this first |
|---|---|
| 1 | A simple progressive program you can show up for |
| 2 | Enough daily protein from food (powder fills gaps) |
| 3 | Sleep and steps that aren’t a disaster |
| 4 | **Then** creatine → protein tub → caffeine as needed |

Supplements don’t replace the first three. Yeh Bro sells honesty, not miracle carts.

## What beginners should skip (for now)

- Proprietary-blend pre-workouts with mystery doses
- “Fat burner” stacks with medical-sounding claims
- Ten amino products that duplicate whey
- Anything pitching AAS, SARMs, peptides, or cycle/PCT funnels — **hard ban**
- Paying 3× AUD/dose for creatine theatre without a real reason

## 30-day beginner checklist

1. Buy plain **monohydrate**; take 3–5 g daily
2. Hit protein from food; add powder only for gaps
3. Pick a caffeine rule (e.g. none after 2 pm if sleep tanks)
4. Train 3–4× / week on a written plan
5. Re-order on **AUD / effective dose**, not hype

## FAQ

**Do I need all three on day one?**  
Creatine alone is a fine start. Add protein powder when food maths fails. Caffeine only if it helps training without wrecking sleep.

**Women?**  
Same stack logic. Same creatine dose range for most. Ignore pink-tax bundles.

**Can I use a mass gainer instead of whey?**  
Different tool (more carbs/calories). Not the default beginner protein pick.
