**Scope:** Australia-first · seed `/go/` only.

## TL;DR

- **Daily protein total** beats overnight casein theatre for most lifters.
- Casein digests slower; whey is typically faster — useful nuance, not a magic hypertrophy switch.
- Overnight casein is **optional** if it helps you hit protein and you like it — not mandatory.
- Lactose issues: same honesty as whey — [Lactose & whey isolate](/blog/lactose-whey-isolate).

## What actually differs

| | Whey | Casein |
|---|---|---|
| Digestion speed (typical talk) | Faster | Slower |
| Common use | Post-training / anytime convenience | Evening / “slow release” marketing |
| AUD / 25 g protein | Often better on WPC | Sometimes pricier — run the formula |
| Beginner default | **Whey** (usually) | Optional add-on |

Neither replaces food. Neither is a detox or fat burner.

## Overnight: useful or marketing?

**Useful when:** you struggle to hit protein without a night serve, prefer a thicker shake, and the AUD / 25 g isn’t silly.

**Marketing when:** the tub claims you’ll “grow while you sleep” if you skip casein, or that whey at night “doesn’t work.”

| Yeh Bro take | |
|---|---|
| Hit daily protein however you’ll comply | Priority |
| Cottage cheese / Greek yoghurt | Real-food casein-ish option for many |
| Dedicated casein powder | Fine if maths + preference win |
| Second specialty tub before creatine | Usually backwards — [Beginner stack](/blog/beginner-supplement-stack-australia) |

## FAQ

**Is whey at night wasted?**  
No. Protein timing windows are overstated for most recreational lifters vs daily totals.

**Casein for cutting?**  
Satiety preference maybe — not a fat-loss drug. No medical weight-loss claims here.

**Lactose and casein?**  
Dairy is still dairy for many people. Isolate/plant paths: [Lactose & whey isolate](/blog/lactose-whey-isolate).

**Women?**  
Same protein maths. Ignore pink overnight tax.
