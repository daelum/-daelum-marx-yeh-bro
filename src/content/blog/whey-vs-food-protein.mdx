**Scope:** Protein strategy · [Start here](/start-here) · supplements only after food basics.

Whey doesn’t build muscle that chicken somehow can’t. It wins on **speed, portability, and cost-per-gram** when food is awkward.

## TL;DR

- Hit your daily target first — [protein targets for lifters](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters).
- **Food-first** for most meals. Powder fills gaps.
- Choose whey (or plant) on **cost per ~25 g protein**, taste you’ll drink, and lactose tolerance — not Instagram flavour drops.
- Skip the tub if you’re already hitting targets with meals.

## What powder is good at

| Job | Powder | Whole food |
|---|---|---|
| Fast gap fill post-training / between meetings | Excellent | Depends on prep |
| Chewing satiety | Weak | Strong |
| Micronutrients / food skills | Weak | Strong |
| Cost per gram (often) | Competitive | Varies |
| Travel / campus | Excellent | Hit-or-miss |

If appetite is low on a cut, a shake can be the difference between hitting protein and quietly under-eating — [cutting without wrecking lifts](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts).

## Who should buy a tub

Buy when:

- You’re short **20–50 g** most days without awkward meal gymnastics
- Schedule is chaotic
- You’re lean bulking and need easy calories + protein — [lean bulk basics](/blog/lean-bulk-basics)

Wait when:

- Training habit isn’t there yet — fix [full body consistency](/blog/beginner-full-body-program) first
- You’re collecting tubs for identity — [beginner stack order](/blog/beginner-supplement-stack-australia)

## Whey concentrate vs isolate vs food

- **Concentrate:** fine default for most.
- **Isolate:** fair if lactose wrecks you — [lactose / isolate](/blog/lactose-whey-isolate).
- **Plant:** works with complete amino attention — [plant protein guide](/blog/best-plant-protein-australia).
- **Food:** still the backbone. Eggs, dairy, meat, fish, tofu/tempeh, high-protein yoghurt, etc.
