Gym forums and group chats invented a parallel medical system: screenshots, “bloodwork ranges,” and confident strangers who have never met you. Call it **cycle lore** — the folk religion of PED (performance-enhancing drug) talk. This page is literacy: why lore feels useful, where it fails, and why **clinical care** is a different category. It is **not** a protocol, not a compound list, and not a sourcing guide. Yeh Bro does not sell or link illegal AAS (anabolic-androgenic steroids), SARMs, or grey peptides.

## TL;DR

- Forum lore optimises for **engagement and certainty**. Clinics optimise for **your** history, labs, and risk.
- “I feel fine” and a Discord emoji are not a lipid panel — [bloodwork for lifters](/blog/bloodwork-for-lifters) · [PED bloodwork literacy](/blog/ped-bloodwork-literacy).
- Harm reduction hierarchy: prefer not escalating → know legal risk where you live → if someone proceeds anyway, **honest clinicians + labs** beat anonymous recipes.
- Legal sports nutrition is a separate lane — creatine/protein — not “on-cycle support” theatre.

## Where lore fails hard

1. **No exam, no meds list, no family history** — strangers can’t see your contraindications.
2. **Confirmation bias** — survivors post PRs; quiet injuries don’t go viral.
3. **Product-quality unknowns** on grey markets — purity is not a meme.
4. **Legal exposure** — high-level: many jurisdictions tightly control AAS and related substances; “research chem” labels aren’t a force field. Not legal advice — verify with official sources (e.g. TGA / Border Force context in Australia) and counsel if needed.
5. **PCT / peptide / “support stack” funnels** that quietly become shopping lists — we refuse that voice. See [what I run beside testosterone](/blog/what-i-run-beside-testosterone) for *education* framing only, and [coming off under care](/blog/ped-coming-off-under-care).

## Clinical care — what that phrase means here

- A **licensed clinician** who knows what you’re doing (honesty beats clever concealment if you’re already in a high-risk lane).
- Labs interpreted in context — not Discord reference ranges cosplay.
- Mental health support when body image or dependency risk spikes — Australia: Lifeline **13 11 14**.
- Decisions about prescription medicines stay inside regulated care — not Telegram.

If someone is asking a coach for hormone protocols, that’s a red flag — [online coach red flags](/blog/online-coach-red-flags).

## Harm-reduction hierarchy (education)

1. Prefer **not** escalating into controlled / unapproved drugs.  
2. Know **legal risk** where you live.  
3. If proceeding despite that: medical honesty + monitoring.  
4. Never treat supplement tubs as organ monitoring.  
5. Plan stopping with a clinician — not a forum “cruise forever” identity.
