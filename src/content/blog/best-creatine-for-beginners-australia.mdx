**Scope:** First creatine tub · plain mono.

## TL;DR

- Buy **creatine monohydrate**. Micronised is fine. Skip HCL/buffered theatre for tub one.
- Dose **3–5 g daily**. Loading optional — most beginners don’t need it.
- Budget win = **AUD per 5 g** after AU shipping, not flavour collabs.

## What beginners actually need

| Need | Answer |
|---|---|
| Form | Creatine **monohydrate** |
| Dose | **3–5 g/day**, rest days included |
| Timing | Whenever you’ll remember |
| Tub size | Big enough that AUD / 5 g looks sane |
| Stack mates | Protein if food undershoots; caffeine optional |

You don’t need a “beginner creatine complex.” That’s usually a blend tax.

Not medical advice. Clinical concerns (e.g. kidney) → talk to a qualified professional first.

## Beginner mistakes to skip

- Loading 20 g once and quitting because of gut noise — split or skip load
- Blaming “bloat fat” in week one — [Does creatine cause bloating?](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating)
- Buying pre scoops instead of a daily mono tub
- Waiting for the perfect brand instead of starting 3–5 g
- Anything AAS/SARM/cycle-adjacent — **hard ban**

## 14-day beginner checklist

1. Buy mono; weigh or trust a ~5 g scoop once
2. Take it daily for two weeks
3. Keep training progressive
4. Ignore day-3 scale drama
5. Re-order on AUD / 5 g, not hype

## FAQ

**Women beginners?**  
Same mono + 3–5 g conversation — [Creatine for women](/blog/creatine-for-women).

**Do I need pills?**  
Powder is usually cheaper per gram. Pills are fine if you’ll actually take them — still count total grams/day.

**Can I mix it in coffee?**  
Yes. Habit > perfection. See also [Caffeine dose for training](/blog/caffeine-dose-training).
