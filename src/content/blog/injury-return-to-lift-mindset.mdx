**Scope:** Recovery mindset · [Yeh Bro System](/start-here) · companions [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style) · [joint-friendly hinges](/blog/joint-friendly-hinge-progressions).

**Not medical advice.** Sharp pain, numbness, trauma, chest symptoms, or anything scary → clinician / physio / emergency pathways. This page is mindset for the boring middle: you’ve been cleared to move, and your ego wants last month’s numbers tomorrow. Hero weeks are how people re-injure the same thing and call it “grinding.”

## TL;DR

- Red flags / sharp pain / neurological symptoms → qualified clinician, not a comment section.
- Regress load, range, or exercise — keep the pattern when possible — [joint-friendly hinges](/blog/joint-friendly-hinge-progressions).
- Measure success by **tolerance and consistency**, not matching your old PR week one.
- Machines/cables are legal — [machine-only templates](/blog/machine-only-hypertrophy-templates).
- Deloads and warmups are part of training — [deload](/blog/deload-week) · [warmup circuit](/blog/warmup-circuit-you-keep).
- Ego maxes are how re-injuries happen — [ego lifting](/blog/ego-lifting-vs-training-age).

## Mindset rules

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Celebrate sessions completed | Compare to pre-injury PRs week one |
| Log pain-free range and **reps in reserve (RIR)** | Train through sharp warning signals |
| Sleep and protein like rehab tools | Stim your way into hero sets — [caffeine](/blog/caffeine-dose-training) |
| Ask an in-person coach if cues help — [hire a coach](/blog/when-to-hire-in-person-coach) | Let a Discord design your medical care |

Comeback speed is not a personality test. The next decade of training matters more than matching a screenshot from before the flare.

## A simple return ladder (conceptual)

1. **Pain-free daily function** basics your clinician clears.  
2. **Pattern regressions** (goblet squat vs heavy back squat; Romanian deadlift vs max deadlift; DB or machine press vs fighting barbell path) — [barbell vs DB bench](/blog/barbell-vs-db-bench-shoulders).  
3. **Volume before intensity** — more easy sets you can repeat, not max singles to prove you’re back. Submaximal progressive overload with honest RIR — [rate of perceived exertion (RPE)](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve).  
4. **Rebuild accessories** with cables/machines if free weights aggravate — [cables](/blog/cable-vs-free-weight-trade-offs) · [machines](/blog/machine-only-hypertrophy-templates).  
5. **Deload early** if flare returns — [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style).  
6. **Only then** chase previous working weights — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload).

Log everything — [training logbook](/blog/training-logbook). The logbook becomes proof you are progressing *this* block, not cosplaying last year’s numbers. Warmups get longer for a while — [warmup before lifting](/blog/warmup-before-lifting). Bands for openers: [resistance bands](/go/bands-amz).

Your timeline may be longer than a neat four-week sketch. That’s normal — hold easy weeks until boring work feels boring again.

## What “cleared to train” actually means

Clearance (when you’ve sought it) usually means you can begin a graded return — not that yesterday’s working weights are automatically safe. Shorten range of motion (ROM) or change implements when end ranges aggravate; leave more RIR if symptoms should settle by the next day; chase session completion before load. Progressive overload resumes *after* tolerance returns.

If you never saw a clinician and symptoms are ambiguous, this guide still argues for conservatism: regress first, load second. That’s opinionated harm-reduction for gym culture, not a diagnosis.

## Ego traps

| Trap | Reframe |
|---|---|
| “If I can’t match old loads, I’m cooked” | Training age includes interruptions. Restarting is skill. |
| Skipping warmups to “feel hardcore” | Cold tissue + fear + ego is a terrible stack. |
| Program hopping for a magic fix | Consistency on a regression beats novelty. |
| Comparing to enhanced timelines on IG | Media literacy — [influencer literacy](/blog/enhanced-influencer-media-literacy). |
| “One big week gets me caught up” | Hero weeks are how weekend warriors collect injuries — [weekend warrior](/blog/weekend-warrior-damage-control). |

**Rate of perceived exertion (RPE) honesty:** if the bar feels like RPE 9 at a weight that used to be RPE 6, believe the bar. Belts and straps don’t replace tissue capacity — [grip / belts](/blog/grip-belts-when-gear-helps).

## Common misconceptions

| Claim | Reality check |
|---|---|
| “Rest until 100% or lose everything” | Graded return sits between total rest and reckless loading. |
| “Pain is weakness leaving the body” | Sharp warning pain is information. Don’t romanticise it. |
| “Machines mean I’m soft” | Machines are tools for capacity. |
| “Forum protocols replace clinicians” | Peers aren’t your care team. |
