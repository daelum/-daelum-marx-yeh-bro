**Scope:** Recovery mindset · [Yeh Bro System](/start-here) · companions [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style) · [joint-friendly hinges](/blog/joint-friendly-hinge-progressions).

**Not medical advice.** Sharp pain, numbness, trauma, chest symptoms, or anything scary → clinician / physio / emergency pathways. This page is mindset for the boring middle: you’ve been cleared to move, and your ego wants last month’s numbers tomorrow.

## TL;DR

- Clearance and a plan beat Reddit diagnoses.  
- Regress patterns → rebuild volume → then load — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload).  
- Machines/cables are legal — [machine-only templates](/blog/machine-only-hypertrophy-templates).  
- Ego maxes are how re-injuries happen — [ego lifting](/blog/ego-lifting-vs-training-age).

## Mindset rules

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Celebrate sessions completed | Compare to pre-injury PRs week one |
| Log pain-free range and RIR | Train through sharp warning signals |
| Sleep and protein like rehab tools | Stim your way into hero sets — [caffeine](/blog/caffeine-dose-training) |
| Ask an in-person coach if cues help — [hire a coach](/blog/when-to-hire-in-person-coach) | Let a Discord design your medical care |

## A simple rebuild arc

1. **Pattern regressions** (e.g. lighter hinges, shorter ROM as advised).  
2. **Volume before intensity** — more easy sets, not max singles.  
3. **Deload early** if flare returns — [deload week](/blog/deload-week) · [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style).  
4. Resume normal progressive plan only when boring work feels boring again — [logbook](/blog/training-logbook).

Warmups get longer for a while — [warmup](/blog/warmup-before-lifting). Bands for openers: [resistance bands](/go/bands-amz).

## Soft close

Comeback speed is not a personality. Protect the next decade of training — [Yeh Bro System](/start-here) · [rest days](/blog/rest-days-vs-active-recovery). Creatine when training resumes — [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).
