You ate sushi and woke up “up two kilos.” That isn’t two kilos of body fat overnight. **Sodium**, carbohydrate, creatine, and hydration shift water. Panic-cutting after one weigh-in is how adherence dies.

## TL;DR

- Daily scale noise is mostly **water/glycogen**, not fat.  
- Trend **weekly averages**; keep waist/photos in the loop — [tracking](/blog/tracking-weight-waist-photos).  
- Creatine can bump weight — keep taking it — [bloat myth](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating) · [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).  
- Don’t crash water or run DIY “deplete” protocols from contest forums — [PED hub](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia) literacy only.

## Common swing sources

| Trigger | What often happens |
|---|---|
| High-sodium restaurant meal | Temporary water retention |
| Carb refeed | Glycogen + water — [refeeds](/blog/refeeds-and-diet-breaks) |
| Starting creatine | Scale bump ≠ fat |
| Hard training + inflammation | Short-term weight blip |
| Alcohol night | Sleep + water chaos — [alcohol tax](/blog/alcohol-training-quiet-tax) |
| Low sleep | Hunger + scale weirdness — [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters) |

## What to do instead of panic

1. Weigh the next 5–7 mornings; average them.  
2. Check waist weekly, not hourly photos under new lighting — [photo lighting honesty](/blog/photo-lighting-honesty-for-lifters).  
3. Keep protein and steps steady — [meal structure](/blog/meal-structure-without-apps) · [steps](/blog/step-targets-without-obsessing-neat).  
4. If the *weekly* trend is wrong for your goal, adjust calories calmly — not salt rituals.

Intra electrolytes on sweaty days ≠ a cut hack — [intra electrolytes](/blog/intra-workout-electrolytes-australia) · [intra carbs](/blog/intra-carbs-for-long-sessions).

## Soft close

Salt isn’t your enemy; impatience is. Read weeks, not mornings — [Start here](/start-here).
