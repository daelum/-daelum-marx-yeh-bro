Most tubs sell vibes. The label sells maths — if you do it. This is a **drill**: copy the table, fill two products, pick the less stupid one. Soft Amazon convenience hops at the end; criteria first.

## TL;DR

- Protein: chase **grams of protein per scoop** and **cost per ~25 g protein**, not tub size flex.  
- Creatine: chase **grams of creatine monohydrate per serve** and daily cost at 3–5 g.  
- Proprietary blends that hide doses → walk away — [proprietary blends](/blog/proprietary-blend-pre-workout).  
- Live convenience examples: [Muscle Nation whey isolate](/go/protein-amz) · [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).

## Red-flag spot check (60 seconds)

- [ ] Blend hides caffeine / creatine mg  
- [ ] “Proprietary matrix” before the useful ingredients  
- [ ] Protein math only works if you count free aminos as whole protein (be suspicious)  
- [ ] Medical cure claims on a sports tub
