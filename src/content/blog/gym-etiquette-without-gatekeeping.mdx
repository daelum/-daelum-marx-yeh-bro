Etiquette isn’t “elite only.” It’s how strangers share finite racks without turning Monday into a territorial documentary. Gatekeeping (“you don’t belong on that bar”) is ego with a clipboard. Basic manners are how everyone gets their [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) in.

## TL;DR

- Re-rack, wipe, don’t curl in the only squat rack during peak hour if alternatives exist.
- Ask before working in; share when sets are long.
- Film without blocking walkways; skip form-policing strangers.
- Beginners belong here — point them to [full body](/blog/beginner-full-body-program) energy, not mockery.

## Floor rules that actually matter

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Re-rack plates and dumbbells | Leave a graveyard for the next human |
| Wipe benches/pads when sweaty | Treat shared vinyl as your towel |
| Work in / rotate politely | Camp on a machine scrolling between sets for 25 minutes |
| Keep chalk mess contained (if allowed) | Powder the postcode |
| Share peak-hour popular stations | Gatekeep “this is my Monday bench” |

Travel gyms and hotel closets have their own chaos — [travel sessions](/blog/hotel-travel-gym-sessions) · [home gym](/blog/home-gym-starter-minimalism) if you want out of the circus.

## Ego etiquette

- Dropping intentional competition deadlifts ≠ dropping every warm-up for TikTok.  
- “Can I work in?” is free. Staring until someone leaves is not a strategy.  
- Unsolicited form lectures from people who [ego lift](/blog/ego-lifting-vs-training-age) help nobody — staff/coaches if someone’s in danger.  
- Don’t harass people about “natty or not” on the floor — [natty literacy](/blog/natty-or-not-physique-standards) · [influencer literacy](/blog/enhanced-influencer-media-literacy).

## Phone, perfume, and playlist

- Film your set, not the stranger’s glutes.  
- One earbud awareness > noise-cancelling oblivion next to a busy rack.  
- Cloud of cologne in the free-weight pit is a choice; choose poorly and people remember.
