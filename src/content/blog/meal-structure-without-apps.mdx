You don’t need a barcode scanner personality to get into shape. Apps help some people. They also become procrastination with decimal places. If tracking makes you consistent, cool. If it makes you avoid dinner, use **structure** instead: repeatable meals, protein on the plate, carbs near training.

## TL;DR

- Default: **3–4 meals** (or 2 bigger + 1 shake) you could run on a workday.
- Each meal: **protein anchor** + carb and/or fat + plants for volume.
- Bias carbs toward the training window when sessions feel flat — [carbs around training](/blog/carbs-around-training).
- Powder is convenience, not a personality — [whey vs food](/blog/whey-vs-food-protein) · [Muscle Nation whey isolate](/go/protein-amz).

## Hand-portion cheat sheet (good enough)

| Need | Rough visual |
|---|---|
| Protein | 1–2 palm-sized portions per meal |
| Carbs | 1–2 cupped hands (more on hard training days) |
| Fats | 1–2 thumbs (oils, nuts, cheese, fatty cuts) |
| Volume veg | Fist(s) — hunger management without living on air |

Adjust up for larger humans / hard training, down in a deeper cut — [cutting guide](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts). This isn’t clinical nutrition advice; it’s a starting scaffold.

## A boring day that works

### Example: train 5–6 pm

| Slot | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | Protein + some carbs | Eggs or yoghurt + fruit/oats |
| Lunch | Protein + carbs + veg | Chicken/beef/tofu + rice + salad |
| Pre-lift snack | Fuel | Banana + yoghurt, or leftovers |
| Post / dinner | Protein + carbs | Same template; bigger if hungry from the session |
| Optional | Gap filler | Shake when food is awkward |

Lean bulk? Nudge portions up slowly — [lean bulk basics](/blog/lean-bulk-basics). Cut? Shrink leisure snacks and liquid calories before you strip training carbs — [hunger on a cut](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut).

## Rules that beat macro theatre

1. **Decide dinner before you’re starving.** Default meals remove 9 pm chaos.
2. **Repeat breakfasts and lunches.** Variety can live at dinner.
3. **Protein first on the plate.** Then carbs. Then extras.
4. **Eat out with a plan:** protein + potato/rice + veg; dessert as a choice, not a trance.
5. **Weigh progress weekly**, not meal-to-meal panic.

## When an app *is* useful

- You genuinely have no idea how much you’re eating
- Contest / tight phase where precision matters *and* you’re already consistent
- Short educational audit (2 weeks), then return to structure

If the app owns your mood, delete it and come back to this page.
