**Weekend warrior** = quiet weekdays, heroic weekends. Life happens. The damage control is not “become a monk” overnight — it’s capping Saturday ego, warming up, and keeping a weekday minimum so Sunday isn’t injury roulette.

## TL;DR

- Add **one short weekday session** if you can — even 30 minutes — [full body](/blog/beginner-full-body-program) or [machines](/blog/machine-only-hypertrophy-templates).  
- Weekend sessions: longer warmup, honest RIR — [warmup circuit](/blog/warmup-circuit-you-keep) · [RPE](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve).  
- Don’t stack max lower + max hike + max beers — [alcohol](/blog/alcohol-training-quiet-tax) · [ego lifting](/blog/ego-lifting-vs-training-age).  
- Sleep Sun night like it’s training — [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters) · [sleep trackers](/blog/sleep-trackers-useful-or-anxiety).

## Damage-control checklist

| Lever | Move |
|---|---|
| Volume | Cap weekend hard sets; save some for a weekday |
| Skill | Practice hinges midweek light — [hinges](/blog/joint-friendly-hinge-progressions) |
| Steps | Walk weeknights — [steps](/blog/step-targets-without-obsessing-neat) |
| Food | Protein anchors even on busy days — [meal structure](/blog/meal-structure-without-apps) · [family food](/blog/partner-family-food-macros) |
| Recovery | If every Monday hurts, deload pattern — [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style) · [injury return](/blog/injury-return-to-lift-mindset) |
