**AAS** (anabolic-androgenic steroids) and fertility come up constantly in gym forums — usually as lore, screenshots, and “just run hCG” cosplay. This page is a **question list** to take to a clinician if fertility matters to you. It is not a treatment plan, not doses, and not a product funnel. Yeh Bro does not sell or source AAS, SARMs, or grey peptides.

## TL;DR

- Exogenous androgens can suppress the body’s own production pathways; fertility impact is a real clinical topic — individual outcomes vary.
- Forums are not reproductive endocrinology — [forum lore vs clinical care](/blog/forum-cycle-lore-vs-clinical-care).
- If you’re planning kids (now or later), ask a **licensed clinician** before you escalate — preferably before anyone “just blasts.”
- Prefer not using controlled / unapproved drugs; know legal risk where you live.

## Why this conversation exists

Gym culture sells visual proof. Some people escalate pharmacologically and only later ask about sperm quality, menstrual disruption, or recovery of natural hormone production. Harm reduction means: **ask early**, honestly, with someone who can order the right tests — not a Discord poll.

Partners’ goals count too. Concealment blocks care.

## Questions worth bringing to a clinician

Use these as prompts — your clinician chooses what’s relevant:

### History & goals

1. We’re trying to conceive in ____ months / years — how should that change risk counselling?  
2. What in my training, body weight, sleep, alcohol, and meds already affects fertility? — [alcohol tax](/blog/alcohol-training-quiet-tax) · [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters)  
3. What should I disclose about past or current AAS / SARM / peptide use so you can advise safely?

### Testing & monitoring (clinician-ordered)

4. Which fertility-related labs or semen analysis (if applicable) make sense for *me*?  
5. How do training, recent illness, or substances confound results?  
6. How often should we re-check if something looks off?

See also conceptual bloodwork literacy: [PED bloodwork literacy](/blog/ped-bloodwork-literacy) — still not a DIY panel.

### If someone has already used

7. What does recovery of the HPTA (hypothalamic–pituitary–gonadal axis) conversation look like under care?  
8. When is referral to endocrinology / fertility specialists appropriate?  
9. What symptoms mean urgent care (severe mood crash, suicidal ideation, acute medical red flags)?

Coming-off framing: [coming off under care](/blog/ped-coming-off-under-care). Mental health: Lifeline **13 11 14** (AU).
