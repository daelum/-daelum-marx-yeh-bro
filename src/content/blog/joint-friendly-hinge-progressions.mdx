The hinge (deadlift family) builds backs and posterior chains — and collects egos. Joint-friendly doesn’t mean “never heavy.” It means **earn positions**, progress load with [RIR](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve), and swap variations when your spine sends invoices.

## TL;DR

- Own hip hinge pattern with light loads before max-effort pulls.  
- Prefer trap-bar / RDL / back-friendly setups when conventional wrecks you.  
- Belt braces — it doesn’t replace bracing — [grip/belts](/blog/grip-belts-when-gear-helps).  
- Pain (sharp, weird, lingering) ≠ normal DOMS — deload / professional care.

**Not medical advice** — get injuries checked.

## Progression ladder (example)

| Stage | Tools | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hip hinge drill, dowel, band pull-through | Pattern |
| 2 | DB / KB RDL | Control, stretch under load |
| 3 | Trap-bar deadlift | Strength with friendlier positions for many |
| 4 | Conventional / sumo as skill allows | Competition patterns if wanted |
| 5 | Deficit / pause / tempo | Advanced — only if recovered |

Warm up the pattern every heavy day — [warmup](/blog/warmup-before-lifting). Log what you did — [logbook](/blog/training-logbook).

## Volume and fatigue

Hinges tax recovery. On [upper/lower](/blog/upper-lower-split), one hard hinge emphasis day + lighter RDL elsewhere beats daily max pulls. Advanced volume: [upper/lower advanced](/blog/upper-lower-advanced-volume). Fried? [Deload](/blog/deload-week) · [rest days](/blog/rest-days-vs-active-recovery).

## Gear that helps (sometimes)

- Chalk / straps when grip fails before hips (rotate raw grip).  
- Belt on top sets once bracing exists.  
- Bands for warm-up pull-throughs — [resistance bands](/go/bands-amz) · [home gym](/blog/home-gym-starter-minimalism).

Creatine supports training quality; it doesn’t fix a rounded max attempt — [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).

## Soft close

Hinge heavy when positions are honest. Regress without shame. Progress the logbook — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) · [Yeh Bro System](/start-here).
