## Myths vs straight answers

| Myth | Straight answer |
|---|---|
| “Creatine is only for men” | No — useful for high-intensity training across sexes |
| “It’ll make me look puffy/fat” | Early scale noise is often water, not fat — see bloat guide |
| “I need a smaller ‘female’ dose” | Most land in the same **3–5 g** daily range |
| “I need a special women’s creatine” | You need **monohydrate** and a clear panel |
| “It messes with hormones like steroids” | That’s not how creatine works — and we don’t do hormone/PCT supplement theatre |

## FAQ

**Will creatine ruin a cut?**  
It can move water weight. Fat loss still tracks energy balance and training. Use tape/photos, not day-2 scale panic.

**Is it safe with the contraceptive pill?**  
General sports-nutrition content isn’t personal medical advice — ask your clinician if you have specific medication questions.

**Vegan women?**  
Creatine is typically synthesised (not extracted from meat) in commercial mono powders — still read the label for your brand.

**Pre-workout instead?**
