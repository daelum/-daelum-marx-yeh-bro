## What creatine actually does (no hype)

Creatine helps top up phosphocreatine stores so you can push a bit harder on short, high-intensity work — heavy sets, sprints, repeated efforts. It is one of the most studied sports supplements. It is **not** a steroid, not a fat burner, and not a shortcut past progressive overload and protein intake.

We care about **label dose**, **AUD economics**, and **AU availability** — not influencer flavour drops.

## Dose that actually matters

| Approach | How | Who it’s for |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily (default)** | 3–5 g monohydrate once daily | Most lifters |
| **Loading (optional)** | ~20 g/day split for 5–7 days, then 3–5 g | People who want saturation faster; not required |
| **Missed a day** | Resume next day — consistency over timing theatre | Everyone |

**Timing:** With a meal or around training is fine. The win is **daily consistency**, not the perfect clock.

**Water / “bloat”:** Creatine can increase intracellular water. That is not the same as body fat. If the scale ticks up a kilo early, that is often the water story.

Not medical advice. If you have a kidney condition or other clinical concern, talk to a qualified professional before supplementing.

## Scoop maths: how to compare tubs in AUD

Ignore “serves per tub” marketing when the scoop isn’t ~3–5 g creatine. Do this instead:

1. Find **grams of creatine monohydrate per serve** on the nutrition panel.
2. Find **price in AUD** (landed if importing — shipping + possible duties).
3. Calculate **AUD per 5 g creatine** (normalise everything to 5 g).

**Shipping honesty:** Prefer local fulfilment when the landed gap is small. “Free shipping” overseas often isn’t free after freight. Prices drift — **always check the retailer**.

## FAQ

**Do I need to cycle creatine?**  
No evidence-based need for most healthy lifters. Daily use is the standard approach.

**Can I mix it in coffee?**  
Yes. Heat in a mug is fine for practical purposes. Taste is chalky either way — micronised helps a bit.

**Will it make me look soft?**  
Early water weight ≠ fat. Keep training and diet honest.

**Is creatine safe?**  
For healthy adults at standard doses, the research base is large. That is **not** medical advice for your individual situation.
