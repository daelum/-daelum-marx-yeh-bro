Creatine monohydrate is still the boring winner. Yeh — fancy forms exist. Most lifters don’t need them.

**TL;DR:** Take **3–5 g creatine monohydrate daily**. Loading is optional. Buy a clear label from an AU stockist you trust. Skip disease claims and “explode your gains” copy.

## What matters in Australia

1. **Form** — Creapure® or generic micronised mono both work if purity checks out.
2. **Dose** — Consensus range is ~3–5 g/day for most adults. More isn’t a personality trait.
3. **Price per effective dose** — AUD per gram of actual creatine, not tub size flex.
4. **Stock & shipping** — Prefer AU retailers when the maths is close.

## Loading?

You can load (~20 g/day split for 5–7 days) to saturate faster. You don’t have to. Daily 3–5 g gets you there — just slower. If your stomach complains on a load, don’t load.

## Common myths

| Claim | Reality |
|---|---|
| “You need HCL or ethyl ester” | Mono has the evidence base. Other forms are usually more $/effective dose. |
| “Creatine is a steroid” | No. It’s a researched sports supplement. |
| “It’ll wreck your kidneys” | Healthy adults using standard doses: not what the literature supports as a scare story — talk to your GP if you have kidney disease. Not medical advice. |
| “Bloat = fat” | Early water weight in muscle cells ≠ body fat. |
