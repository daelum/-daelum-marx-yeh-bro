A **calorie** (technically a kilocalorie on labels) is a unit of **energy**. Your body uses that energy to stay alive, move, digest food, and recover from training. Cut vs bulk is mostly about whether you’re a bit under or over what you burn across weeks — not whether rice is “evil” after 6 pm.

## TL;DR

- Energy in vs energy out drives fat change over **weeks**, with water/glycogen noise day to day.  
- **Deficit** ≈ lose fat (if honest and sustained). **Surplus** ≈ gain weight (hopefully some muscle if you train).  
- **Macros** (protein, carbs, fat) are how you *split* calories — they don’t repeal energy balance.

## Cut vs bulk without mysticism

| Goal | Energy story | Lifter job |
|---|---|---|
| Hold weight | Near **maintenance** | Build skill, sleep, protein habit |
| Lose fat | Modest **deficit** | Keep training quality — [cutting guide](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts) |
| Grow | Small **surplus** | Don’t dirty-bulk the cut away — [lean bulk](/blog/lean-bulk-basics) |

The scale lies short-term: sodium, carbs, creatine water, hormones, stress. Use trends + waist/photos — [tracking weight / waist / photos](/blog/tracking-weight-waist-photos) · [sodium / scale panic](/blog/sodium-water-scale-panic).

## Where “calories out” comes from (first-pass glossary)

You’ll see these everywhere — expand once, then move on:

| Term | Plain English |
|---|---|
| **BMR** / **RMR** | Basal / resting metabolic rate — energy to stay alive at rest (formulas estimate; they’re not lab truth) |
| **TEF** | Thermic effect of food — digesting/processing what you eat (protein costs a bit more than fat) |
| **NEAT** | Non-exercise activity thermogenesis — fidgeting, steps, standing, chores |
| **EAT** | Exercise activity — the gym, sport, hard cardio |
| **TDEE** | Total daily energy expenditure — roughly the sum of the above |

You don’t need a PhD. You need a workable **maintenance** estimate, then a nudge. Full method: [estimate maintenance calories](/blog/estimate-maintenance-calories).
