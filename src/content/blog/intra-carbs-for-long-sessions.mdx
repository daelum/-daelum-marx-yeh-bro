**Intra-workout carbs** (carbohydrate taken during the session) are useful for some long, glycolytic training days. They are optional for most 45–75 minute gym sessions if you ate beforehand. This is decision literacy — not a soft-drink sponsorship.

## TL;DR

- Under ~60–75 minutes with a solid pre-meal: **water ± electrolytes** usually enough.  
- Long volume days, multiple sessions, or you train fasted and fade: small easy carbs can help.  
- Intra carbs aren’t a fat-loss hack or a bulk requirement.  
- Electrolytes ≠ carbs — see [intra electrolytes](/blog/intra-workout-electrolytes-australia).

## When intra carbs earn the slot

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| 90+ minute high-volume sessions | Glycogen + blood glucose comfort |
| Two-a-days / long powerbuilding days | — [powerbuilding](/blog/powerbuilding-hybrid-week) |
| Hot gym + heavy sweat | Pair with fluids/electrolytes |
| Fasted training that always dies at set six | Or just eat pre-session — [meal structure](/blog/meal-structure-without-apps) |

## When to skip

- Short sessions with a normal pre-meal  
- Deep cut where you’re chasing every millilitre of liquid calories without a performance need — fix the cut — [cutting](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts) · [hunger](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut)  
- Using sugary drinks as a personality between sets while ignoring protein and sleep  

## Practical options (food first)

| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| Fruit / simple starch before or during long sessions | Easy digestion beats gut bombs |
| Dilute sports drink | Watch total sugar vs need |
| Plain water + electrolytes | Often enough — [electrolytes guide](/blog/intra-workout-electrolytes-australia) |

Place most carbs around the session across the day — [carbs around training](/blog/carbs-around-training). Protein still sits in meals/shakes — [Muscle Nation whey isolate](/go/protein-amz) if convenient. Creatine remains daily, not “intra only” — [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).

Stim note: caffeine can pair with long sessions but late stims wreck sleep — [caffeine dose](/blog/caffeine-dose-training) · [pre without hype](/blog/pre-workout-without-hype).

## Soft close

Intra carbs are a tool for long work, not a membership identity. Eat around training; sip with purpose — [Start here](/start-here).
