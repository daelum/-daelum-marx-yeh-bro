## Goal of this phase

Do **not** jump from a hard deficit into a high-stimulus surplus (or into culture talk about higher androgen exposure). Stabilise the new weight, restore calories, and treat health markers as a **gate**.

## Diet — maintenance reset

Bring calories back to estimated **maintenance** at the new body weight. That helps metabolic rate and hunger hormones (leptin / ghrelin) settle after a cut.

Practical cues:

- Add food slowly over 1–2 weeks rather than slamming a huge surplus day one
- Keep protein high enough that training feels recoverable
- Watch scale + waist; maintenance is a range, not a single calorie spell

## Why this phase exists in enhanced culture

Forum lore often keeps a “baseline” androgen discussion flat through this window and insists on **labs before escalating**. Agree with the **gate idea** as harm-reduction literacy — a fitness brand does not replace a doctor.

**Bloodwork-before-escalation** is the headline of Phase 2. Skipping it is how “I feel fine” becomes a late lipid or haematocrit surprise.

## Bloodwork & BP — concepts clinicians use (not a DIY panel)

Ordering and interpreting labs is a **medical** act. We won’t publish “run this panel then blast.” Here’s **why** people care about categories clinicians commonly discuss:

| Marker cluster (talked about in culture / clinics) | Why it comes up |
|---|---|
| Lipids (HDL, LDL, triglycerides) | Androgens and diet can shift cardiovascular risk markers |
| Haematocrit / haemoglobin | Androgens can raise red-cell mass; viscosity / BP risk chatter |
| Liver enzymes (ALT, AST) & kidney context (eGFR, creatinine) | Stress / confounders / compound class risk — clinician interprets |
| Blood pressure | Home trends matter; culture often cites staying under ~120/80 as a “green” chat target — **your** clinician sets thresholds |

If readings or symptoms worry you: **see a doctor**. Emergency symptoms (chest pain, severe mood change, etc.) → urgent care.

## Chemical talk (literacy only)

Enhanced culture often keeps discussing the same **~100–150 mg/week** “baseline” ester patterns through this consolidation window. That is **forum pattern language**, not Yeh Bro writing your protocol. No sourcing. No pin schedule as site voice.

## Exit criteria before Phase 3

- Weight stable at maintenance
- Clinician-reviewed markers you personally need — not a screenshot Discord “clear”
- BP and symptoms not screaming red flags
- Only then does culture talk about higher anabolic stimulus — still **not** this site prescribing it
