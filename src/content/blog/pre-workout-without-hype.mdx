If the label hides doses behind a **proprietary blend**, you’re guessing. Fair call: skip it.

For evening / caffeine-free options see [Best stim-free pre-workout Australia](/blog/best-stim-free-pre-workout-australia).

## TL;DR

You need a stimulant plan you understand (often caffeine), not a firework tub. Clear doses > neon marketing. Or use coffee and save the money for creatine and protein.

## Red flags

- Proprietary blend with no per-ingredient milligrams
- “Like steroids but natural” energy (hard ban on this site)
- Tiny font stimulant stacks you can’t total
- Before/after photos implying powder = AAS-level change

<div class="skip-flag">

**Skip / red flag:** mystery blends, disease claims, cycle talk. Yeh Bro stays on legal sports nutrition only.

</div>

## What “enough” looks like (general)

- **Caffeine:** know your total mg from the scoop + any other sources
- **Citrulline / beta-alanine / creatine:** only useful if the dose is real — which means it must be listed
- **Creatine in a pre:** fine if dosed; still easier as a cheap separate mono

This is training nutrition talk, not medical advice. If stimulants aren’t for you, don’t force a pre — try [stim-free](/blog/best-stim-free-pre-workout-australia) or coffee.

## AU shopping habit

1. Open the label PDF or panel
2. Add up caffeine
3. Check whether “pump” ingredients have real doses
4. Compare $/serve to coffee + creatine
