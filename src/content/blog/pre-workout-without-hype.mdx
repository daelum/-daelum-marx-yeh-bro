If the label hides doses behind a **proprietary blend**, you’re guessing. Fair call: skip it. Pre-workout can be a useful tool — caffeine you understand, maybe a few actives at real doses — or it can be a neon tub that sells vibes and hides the milligrams. Yeh Bro’s bar is simple: **read the panel, total the stimulants, compare the math to coffee**.

For evening / caffeine-free options see [Best stim-free pre-workout Australia](/blog/best-stim-free-pre-workout-australia).

## TL;DR

- You need a **stimulant plan you understand** (often caffeine), not a firework tub.
- Clear per-ingredient milligrams beat marketing. Proprietary blends that hide mg → walk.
- Or use coffee and put the money into [creatine](/blog/best-creatine-australia) and [protein](/blog/protein-scoop-economics-australia).
- Stack order for most beginners still isn’t “more pre.” It’s training, sleep, protein, creatine, then caffeine as optional.

## Red flags

- Proprietary blend with no per-ingredient milligrams — [proprietary blend guide](/blog/proprietary-blend-pre-workout)
- “Like steroids but natural” energy (hard ban on this site)
- Tiny-font stimulant stacks you can’t total across coffee + pre + soft drinks
- Before/after photos implying powder = anabolic-steroid-level change
- Fat-burner medical claims taped onto a training powder

## What “enough” looks like (general)

This is training-nutrition talk, not a prescription. Tolerance varies; if stimulants aren’t for you, don’t force a pre.

| Ingredient / idea | Yeh Bro take |
|---|---|
| **Caffeine** | Know total mg from the scoop **plus** coffee, tea, soft drinks, gum. Labels beat vibes — [caffeine dose](/blog/caffeine-dose-training). |
| **Citrulline** | Only useful if the dose is listed and real. Hidden “pump matrix” = marketing. |
| **Beta-alanine** | Tingles ≠ gains proof. Dose must be listed — [beta-alanine tingles](/blog/beta-alanine-tingles). |
| **Creatine in a pre** | Fine if dosed; still cheaper and clearer as separate monohydrate daily. |
| **“Focus blends”** | Often under-dosed noise. If you can’t total actives, skip. |

If you’re training late, protect sleep first — try [stim-free](/blog/best-stim-free-pre-workout-australia) or move coffee earlier. A wrecked night of sleep costs more performance than one skipped scoop.

## AU shopping habit

1. Open the label PDF or panel (retailer page counts).
2. Add up caffeine — scoop + anything else you’ll drink that day.
3. Check whether “pump” / “focus” ingredients have real, listed doses.
4. Compare **$ per serve** to coffee + a cheap creatine tub.
5. If the brand won’t show milligrams, don’t invent trust. Leave.

Promotions change weekly. Do the maths the day you buy.

## Common mistakes

- Scooping a huge pre on top of a large coffee “because it’s gym day”
- Buying the loudest tub instead of the clearest label
- Using pre to paper over under-sleep and under-eating
- Expecting a powder to replace [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload)

## Takeaways

Clear doses beat neon. Know your caffeine total. Skip mystery blends. Put beginner budget into creatine and protein first; treat pre as optional session seasoning — or just drink coffee.
