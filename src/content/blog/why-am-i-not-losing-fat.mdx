If the scale won’t trend down for 2–4 weeks on a “deficit,” one of three things is usually true: you’re **not actually in a deficit**, **water/glycogen is masking fat loss**, or the deficit is so nasty that **NEAT** (non-exercise activity thermogenesis — daily movement outside the gym) collapsed and hunger exploded. Rarely is it a secret organ failure you diagnose on TikTok.

## TL;DR

- Audit intake honesty first — [calorie tracking mistakes](/blog/calorie-tracking-mistakes).  
- Watch steps / fidgeting — **NEAT** often falls hard on cuts — [step targets](/blog/step-targets-without-obsessing-neat).  
- Separate fat loss from scale panic — [weight / waist / photos](/blog/tracking-weight-waist-photos).  
- Fix rate and adherence before shopping fat burners — [hunger noise vs need](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut).

## Stall checklist (run in order)

| Check | What to look for | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reporting | Oils, bites, drinks, weekend blowouts “not counting” | 7–14 day honest log — [tracking mistakes](/blog/calorie-tracking-mistakes) |
| 2. Maintenance guess | Target was never below true **TDEE** (total daily energy expenditure) | Re-estimate — [maintenance](/blog/estimate-maintenance-calories) |
| 3. NEAT crash | Steps halved; desk-frozen evenings | Walk baseline back — [walking](/blog/walking-for-lifters) |
| 4. Water noise | High sodium / carbs / creatine start / stress | Trend + waist — [sodium panic](/blog/sodium-water-scale-panic) |
| 5. Deficit too sharp | Lifts dying, sleep trash, constant fridge orbit | Raise calories; slow the cut — [cutting guide](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts) |
| 6. Phase length | Expected stage-lean in three weeks | Patience + [refeeds / diet breaks](/blog/refeeds-and-diet-breaks) if needed |

## Hunger is part of the system

A real deficit makes you hungry. That’s physiology, not moral failure. Answer **need** (protein/fuel) and manage **noise** (habit snacking) — full split: [hunger on a cut](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut). Protein and volume food help; stim stacks don’t create a deficit — [energy drinks vs pre in a deficit](/blog/energy-drinks-vs-pre-workout-deficit).

## “Metabolic damage” theatre

Adaptation is real: you get a bit more efficient, **NEAT** drops, hormones shift a little when lean and underfed. That’s not a license for untracked “refeeds” every other night. It’s a reason to:

1. Keep deficits **moderate**  
2. Protect sleep and steps  
3. Plan exits — [reverse diet literacy](/blog/reverse-diet-literacy) · [cut-to-bulk mistakes](/blog/cut-to-bulk-transition-mistakes)  
4. Recalculate as weight falls — [recalculating calories](/blog/recalculating-calories-after-weight-change)

Not medical advice — persistent fatigue, amenorrhea, or clinical red flags → clinician, not a forum stack.
