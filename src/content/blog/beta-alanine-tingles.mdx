## What the tingles are (boring version)

Beta-alanine can cause a temporary pins-and-needles sensation for some people, especially at higher single doses. It’s usually talked about as **paresthesia** — uncomfortable for some, fine for others.

| True | False |
|---|---|
| Can feel weird on skin/face | “Tingles mean max gains” |
| Often dose-related | “No tingles = fake product” |
| Fades for many with time/split doses | “Must be in every pre” |

Not medical advice. Unusual or severe reactions → stop and talk to a professional.

## When to skip it

| Skip if… | Fair to try if… |
|---|---|
| You hate the sensation | You tolerate it and train hard/repeated efforts |
| Pre hides the mg in a proprietary blend | Dose is labelled and you chose it on purpose |
| You’re a beginner still sorting protein + creatine | Stack basics are already locked |
| Sleep/anxiety already messy and the pre is stim-stacked | You’re using a calmer evening formula |

## Label literacy

1. Is **beta-alanine** listed with a **mg/g amount**?
2. Is it buried in a “Pump/Endurance Matrix”? → usually **skip** ([Proprietary blend pre-workout](/blog/proprietary-blend-pre-workout)).
3. Are you double-dosing across pre + separate beta-alanine tubs without noticing?

## FAQ

**Do tingles mean it’s absorbing?**  
They mean you’re sensing a known side effect for some users — not a trophy.

**Can I split the dose?**  
Many people split across the day when using standalone beta-alanine — follow the product label; still not personal medical advice.

**Women?**  
Same sensation rules. No gendered tingle tax.

**Is this the same as caffeine jitters?**  
No. Different ingredient, different feel.
