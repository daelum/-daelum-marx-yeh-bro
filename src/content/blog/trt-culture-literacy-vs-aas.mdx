Gym language smears **TRT** (testosterone replacement therapy under clinical care) into the same bucket as **AAS** (anabolic-androgenic steroid) blast/cruise folklore. That smear helps marketers and hurts decision-making. This page is literacy: how the lanes differ in conversation, why DIY “TRT” from a Telegram kit is not care, and what belongs with a clinician. **No doses, no sources, no cycle design.**

## TL;DR

- Clinical TRT is a **medical** pathway (diagnosis, monitoring, prescription legality where you live).  
- AAS culture is often **supraphysiological** enhancement talk — different intent and risk profile in harm-reduction education.  
- Calling a blast “TRT” doesn’t make it clinical — [forum lore vs clinical care](/blog/forum-cycle-lore-vs-clinical-care).  
- Yeh Bro does not sell or source testosterone, AAS, or grey peptides.

## How gym talk confuses the words

| Phrase you’ll hear | Literacy read |
|---|---|
| “I’m on TRT” | Could mean clinician-guided care — or DIY enhancement cosplay |
| “Cruise” | AAS-culture maintenance language — not automatically medical TRT |
| “Just replace what you shut down” | Forum story; not your prescription |
| Support tubs as “TRT essentials” | Shopping theatre — [support-stack education](/blog/what-i-run-beside-testosterone) |

## Clinical lane (high-level)

Legitimate care involves assessment, labs, informed consent, and follow-up — [bloodwork for lifters](/blog/bloodwork-for-lifters) · [PED bloodwork literacy](/blog/ped-bloodwork-literacy). Fertility goals change counselling — [fertility + AAS questions](/blog/fertility-aas-clinician-questions). Mental health and dependency risk deserve honesty — Lifeline **13 11 14** (AU).

Legal status of androgens varies; possession/supply/import can be serious. Not legal advice — verify official sources.

## AAS-culture lane (high-level)

Enhancement patterns discussed in PED education include cardiovascular, endocrine, hepatic (especially some orals — [oral AAS liver literacy](/blog/oral-aas-liver-risk-literacy)), and psychosocial risks. Prefer not escalating. If someone proceeds despite that, medical honesty beats Discord.
