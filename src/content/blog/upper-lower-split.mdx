**Scope:** Training sequel · [Yeh Bro System · training](/start-here#step-2) · after [beginner full body](/blog/beginner-full-body-program).

You’ve run full body for a while. The lifts feel less scary. You’re hungry for a bit more volume on chest, back, or legs without living in the gym six days a week. **Upper / lower** is the boring next step most people should take — not a 7-day “bro split” with zero recovery.

## TL;DR

- Upper / lower = **four sessions**: Upper A, Lower A, Upper B, Lower B (or two of each).
- Graduate when **3× full body** is consistent for 8–12+ weeks and you want more pattern volume — not because a reel said so.
- Keep [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) and **1–3 reps in reserve** (see [RPE guide](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve)).
- If life only allows three days, stay on full body — [how many days](/blog/how-many-days-to-lift).

## When upper / lower earns the slot

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| You’re hitting most planned sessions | Consistency exists |
| Big patterns feel skilled, not terrifying | Practice bank is real |
| Full-body days feel rushed before you finish useful accessories | Volume hunger is legitimate |
| Sleep and food are roughly in place | Recovery can absorb four days |

**Not** a reason to switch: boredom after two weeks, influencer periodisation cosplay, or chasing “optimisation” while still missing Monday.

## The week shape

Classic recoverable layout:

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Upper A |
| Tue | Lower A |
| Wed | Walk / off |
| Thu | Upper B |
| Fri | Lower B |
| Sat–Sun | Walk, easy life, optional light conditioning |

Swap days to fit work. Keep **at least one day** between same-pattern hits when you can. Fried for a week? [Deload](/blog/deload-week) — don’t invent a new split.

## Templates (patterns over brand of rack)

### Upper A — horizontal bias

| Pattern | Example | Sets × reps |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal push | Bench or dumbbell press | 3–4 × 5–8 |
| Horizontal pull | Row variation | 3–4 × 6–10 |
| Vertical push | Overhead press | 2–3 × 6–10 |
| Vertical pull | Pulldown or pull-up progression | 2–3 × 6–10 |
| Arms / side delts (optional) | Curl + lateral raise | 2 × 10–15 |

### Lower A — squat bias

| Pattern | Example | Sets × reps |
|---|---|---|
| Squat | Back / front / goblet / leg press | 3–4 × 5–8 |
| Hinge assist | Romanian deadlift | 2–3 × 6–10 |
| Single-leg or lunge | Split squat | 2–3 × 8–12 |
| Carry / core | Farmer or suitcase carry | 2–3 rounds |

### Upper B — vertical / pump bias

| Pattern | Example | Sets × reps |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical push | Overhead press (main) | 3–4 × 5–8 |
| Vertical pull | Pull-up / pulldown | 3–4 × 5–8 |
| Horizontal push (lighter) | Incline or machine | 2–3 × 8–12 |
| Horizontal pull (lighter) | Cable / chest-supported | 2–3 × 8–12 |
| Rear delt / arm finish | Face pull + triceps | 2 × 10–15 |

### Lower B — hinge bias

| Pattern | Example | Sets × reps |
|---|---|---|
| Hinge | Trap-bar or conventional (conservative) | 3 × 3–6 |
| Squat assist | Leg press or hack | 2–3 × 8–12 |
| Ham / glute accessory | Leg curl or hip thrust | 2–3 × 8–12 |
| Calves / core | As needed | 2–3 × 8–15 |

Warm up like an adult — [warmup before lifting](/blog/warmup-before-lifting). Leave ego sets for Instagram, not the logbook.

## Volume without frying yourself

- Most work sets: stop with **reps in reserve** — [RPE / RIR](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve).
- Progress load or reps on the **main** lifts first; accessories can coast.
- Four hard days plus daily redline cardio is how intermediates stall. Walk instead — [walking for lifters](/blog/walking-for-lifters).
