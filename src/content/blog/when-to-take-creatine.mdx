## TL;DR

- **Best time = the time you’ll actually take it every day.**
- Post-workout-only rules are mostly theatre for recreational lifters.
- Take **3–5 g monohydrate** on training **and** rest days.
- Loading optional — [Creatine loading phase](/blog/creatine-loading-phase) · full protocol [How to dose creatine](/blog/how-to-dose-creatine).

## Myths vs what matters

| Myth | Straight answer |
|---|---|
| “Only post-workout or it doesn’t work” | Daily total + consistency matter more |
| “Morning only / night only” | Pick a habit anchor |
| “Must take with 100 g carbs” | Nice context in some protocols; not a hard mono requirement |
| “Skip rest days” | Don’t — saturation is daily |
| “Cycle 8 on / 4 off” | No evidence-based need for most healthy lifters |

Tie the scoop to coffee, a shake, or dinner. Habit > stopwatch.

## Practical timing options

| Anchor | Why it works |
|---|---|
| With breakfast / coffee | Easy daily cue; mono in coffee is fine for practical use |
| Around training | Fine if that’s when you remember |
| With a protein shake | Convenient — but if dairy wrecks you, don’t confuse lactose with creatine ([Lactose & whey isolate](/blog/lactose-whey-isolate)) |
| Evening | OK if it doesn’t upset your gut/sleep routine |

Water/scale noise ≠ failed timing — [Does creatine cause bloating?](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating).

## Missed doses and travel

- Missed a day → resume tomorrow.
- Travel → zip bag or small tub; airport rules vary — plan ahead.
- Don’t “catch up” with a 20 g dump unless you’re intentionally on a split loading plan.

## FAQ

**Pre-workout creatine scoop enough?**  
Only if it reliably hits ~3–5 g **every day**. Most pres don’t replace a dedicated mono tub.

**With coffee — does heat destroy it?**  
For practical daily use, mixing in coffee is fine.

**Fasted training?**  
You can still take creatine whenever you’ll comply; fasted preference ≠ creatine failure.

**Loading timing?**  
If you load, split across the day — [Creatine loading phase](/blog/creatine-loading-phase).
