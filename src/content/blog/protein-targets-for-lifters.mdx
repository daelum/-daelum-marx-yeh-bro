**Scope:** Nutrition basics · [Start here](/start-here) step 3.

Protein matters. Obsessing over ±3 grams while sleeping five hours doesn’t. Here’s the range I’d actually use.

## TL;DR

- Practical lifter range: about **1.6–2.2 g protein per kg** body weight most days.
- **Food first.** Powder fills gaps — [whey vs food](/blog/whey-vs-food-protein).
- Hit the target **most days**, not perfectly forever.
- In a hard cut, many people sit toward the **higher** end for satiety and muscle retention — [cutting without wrecking lifts](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts).

## The range, without the mythology

| Situation | Practical target |
|---|---|
| Building the habit | ~**1.6–2.0 g/kg** |
| Serious lifting / recomp | ~**1.6–2.2 g/kg** |
| Aggressive fat loss | Often **~2.0–2.5 g/kg** (food maths, not medical dosing) |

If you have kidney disease or another medical condition, talk to a clinician or accredited dietitian before high-protein experiments. Not medical advice.

Body weight for the maths: use **scale weight** for most people. If you’re carrying a lot of body fat, some coaches use an adjusted weight — don’t spiral; pick a sane number and be consistent.

## How to hit it without living in an app

1. **Anchor meals** — include a clear protein source each meal (meat, fish, eggs, dairy, tofu/tempeh, Legumes + pairing as needed).
2. **Repeat breakfasts / lunches** you already like so the grams become automatic.
3. **Use a shake when food is awkward** — travel, campus, long gaps — not as a personality.
4. Track for **1–2 weeks** if you’re guessing wild. Then loosen the leash once the pattern is obvious.

Powder buying criteria when you need convenience: [Best protein powder](/blog/best-protein-powder-australia) · [/go/protein-true](/go/protein-true) · [/go/protein-bulk](/go/protein-bulk).

## Distribution — useful, not sacred

Spreading protein across **3–5 feedings** is usually easier than one giant dinner. You don’t need a stopwatch “anabolic window.” Training days: a solid meal or shake around the session is convenient, not magic.

Casein-at-night theatre: optional. See [casein vs whey](/blog/casein-vs-whey) if you’re curious — most people just need the daily total.

## Obsession red flags

- Skipping social meals over 10 g variance
- Fear of plant or dairy protein because of forum lore
- Buying five tubs before the training habit exists — [beginner stack order](/blog/beginner-supplement-stack-australia)
- Ignoring calories entirely while micromanaging protein (you still need enough energy to train)
