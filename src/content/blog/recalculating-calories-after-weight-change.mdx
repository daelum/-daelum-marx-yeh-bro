Your **TDEE** (total daily energy expenditure) isn’t a tattoo. Lose 8 kg and the old maintenance is often too high for a continued cut. Gain on a bulk and the old surplus can stall. Recalculate on purpose — not every noisy Wednesday.

## TL;DR

- Revisit targets after ~**3–5%+** body-weight change, or when the 2–4 week trend dies.  
- Re-estimate maintenance, then re-apply cut/bulk % — [estimate maintenance](/blog/estimate-maintenance-calories).  
- Update protein grams to current body weight — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters).  
- Rule out logging lies before you slash harder — [tracking mistakes](/blog/calorie-tracking-mistakes) · [why am I not losing?](/blog/why-am-i-not-losing-fat).

## When to recalculate

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Weight down meaningfully on a cut | Lower calories *slightly* or raise steps; don’t crash |
| Weight up on a lean bulk, surplus gone | Add ~100–200 kcal and reassess in 2 weeks |
| Stall with honest logging | Confirm intake → adjust 5–10% |
| Exit cut → reverse / bulk | Climb with a plan — [reverse diet](/blog/reverse-diet-literacy) · [cut-to-bulk mistakes](/blog/cut-to-bulk-transition-mistakes) |

Use weekly averages + waist/photos — [tracking weight / waist / photos](/blog/tracking-weight-waist-photos). Creatine or high-carb days can fake jumps — [sodium / scale panic](/blog/sodium-water-scale-panic).

## Simple update recipe

1. Note average intake of the last 14 honest days.  
2. Note weight trend.  
3. Infer current maintenance (flat → intake ≈ maintenance; falling → intake below, etc.).  
4. Set new phase target.  
5. Refresh macro split — [macro split basics](/blog/macro-split-basics-for-lifters) · fat floor — [dietary fat floors](/blog/dietary-fat-floors-for-lifters).

Hand-portion people: recalibrate palms occasionally with a scale week — [tracking vs hand portions](/blog/tracking-vs-hand-portions).
