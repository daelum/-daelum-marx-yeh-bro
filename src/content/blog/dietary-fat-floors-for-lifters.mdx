**Scope:** Food literacy · hub [calories & macros](/blog/calories-and-macros-for-lifters) · [macro split basics](/blog/macro-split-basics-for-lifters) · [Yeh Bro System · food](/start-here#step-3). **Not medical advice** — hormones and clinical care belong with clinicians.

Dietary **fat** is calorie-dense (~9 kcal/g) and easy to overshoot in a dirty bulk — and easy to nuke in a panic cut. Lifters usually want a **floor**: enough fat for food quality, fat-soluble vitamins, and not living on dry chicken cosplay. Ultra-low-fat “testosterone hacks” are mostly forum theatre.

## TL;DR

- Keep a practical fat band (many coaches use ~**0.6–1.0 g/kg** as a starting scaffold — individual, not a prescription).  
- Cut calories from snacks and oils before you delete every yolk.  
- Hormone talk stays **literacy-level** here — symptoms → clinician, not Reddit panels.  
- Pair with [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters) and [carb totals vs timing](/blog/carb-totals-vs-timing-for-lifters).

## Why a floor exists

| Reason | Plain English |
|---|---|
| Adherence | Meals taste like food; diet lasts |
| Vitamins A/D/E/K | Fat helps absorb them from real meals |
| Hormones (context) | Extreme deficits + very low fat + low body fat can correlate with funky labs/symptoms in some people — correlation ≠ your DIY protocol |
| Training life | Sauces, nuts, dairy, fatty fish make the week runnable |

This is **not** “eat butter to boost T.” Enhanced / TRT / AAS contexts are separate literacy lanes — [PED harm reduction](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia) · [bloodwork for lifters](/blog/bloodwork-for-lifters).

## Cutting without zero-fat cosplay

1. Hit protein.  
2. Keep the fat floor.  
3. Pull carbs from leisure first; protect training carbs — [carbs around training](/blog/carbs-around-training).  
4. Measure oils — the silent surplus/deficit killer — [calorie tracking mistakes](/blog/calorie-tracking-mistakes).

Hunger management still beats micromanaging every gram of olive oil — [hunger on a cut](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut).
