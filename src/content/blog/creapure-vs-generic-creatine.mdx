## TL;DR

- **Creapure** is a **branded creatine monohydrate** supply chain — not a different magic molecule class.
- **Generic micronised mono** from a reputable AU stockist is enough for most lifters **if** the dose is clear and the source looks credible.

| | Creapure-branded mono | Generic micronised mono |
|---|---|---|

| Typical dose | Still **3–5 g/day** for most | Same |

Fair call: Creapure often costs more per gram. That can be rational if you want the supply-chain story. It is **not** required for creatine to “work.”

**Shipping honesty:** Local generic that ships fast can beat an imported Creapure tub after freight. Always land the price.

## When paying extra makes sense

| Pay up if… | Skip the premium if… |
|---|---|
| You specifically want Creapure on the panel | You’re new and just need 3–5 g/day habit |
| Two tubs are close on AUD / 5 g after shipping | Premium is 2–3× with the same mono dose |
| You distrust ultra-cheap no-name imports | The cheap tub has clear mono grams + sane retailer |

**Not reasons:** “Absorbs like HCL,” “no bloat guaranteed,” “steroid-like fullness.” Banned hype.
