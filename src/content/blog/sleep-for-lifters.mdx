**Scope:** Recovery · [Start here](/start-here) step 4.

You can out-train a bad night once. You can’t out-train a bad month. Sleep is where [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) gets paid.

## TL;DR

- Treat **7–9 hours** as training support, not lifestyle fluff.
- Same bedtime most nights beats heroic weekend catch-up.
- If sleep is trashed for days, **cut volume** or [deload](/blog/deload-week) — don’t add stims and pray.
- Magnesium / “sleep stacks” are optional footnotes, not a substitute for a dark room and a real bedtime.

## What sleep does for the logbook

Poor sleep stacks with:

- Weaker performance and worse reps in reserve judgment
- Higher perceived effort for the same weights
- Hunger and craving noise on a cut — [cutting guide](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts)
- Mood and stress reactivity — [stress and lifting](/blog/stress-and-lifting)

That’s why Start here puts sleep before deep culture reading.

## Build a window you’ll keep

1. Pick a **wake time** you actually need.
2. Count back **7.5–9 hours** for lights-out.
3. Protect the last hour: dimmer lights, less scroll combat, caffeine cutoff that matches your sensitivity — [caffeine timing](/blog/caffeine-dose-training).
4. Keep the room dark, cool-ish, and boring.

Travel and shift work break the ideal — then you manage damage, not fantasy. Catch sleep where you can without turning every night into a debate.

## Training adjustments when sleep tanks

| Situation | Move |
|---|---|
| One rough night | Train, but leave more reps in reserve |
| Several rough nights | Drop a set or two across the week |
| A wrecked week | Formal [deload](/blog/deload-week) |
| Chronic insomnia / apnea signs | Talk to a clinician — not a fitness blog |

Ambition without recovery is how people quit in month three.

## Supplements — keep the hierarchy honest

Order of operations still wins:

1. Bedtime and light hygiene
2. Alcohol honesty (it fragments sleep for a lot of people)
3. Then optional helpers with conservative expectations — e.g. magnesium literacy in [magnesium for lifters](/blog/magnesium-for-lifters-australia) · [/go/mag-iherb](/go/mag-iherb)

Creatine timing myths don’t override sleep — [when to take creatine](/blog/when-to-take-creatine).

## Walks and wind-down

An evening walk can help some people downshift — [walking for lifters](/blog/walking-for-lifters). Hard late HIIT plus a huge late meal is a different story. Experiment; don’t dogma.
