## Goal of this phase

Add muscle with a **conservative** surplus after you are leaner and (if pharmacology is in the picture at all) after health gates from Phase 2 — not a dirty bulk that undoes the cut.

Natural lifters: training and food here still apply. Enhanced culture paragraphs are literacy, not a shopping list.

## Nutrition for lean tissue growth (actionable)

| Lever | Practical target |
|---|---|
| Calories | About a **10% surplus** above maintenance — often ~250–350 kcal/day for many adults |
| Rate of gain | Roughly **0.25–0.5 kg (0.5–1 lb) per week** — faster usually means more fat |
| Protein | About **2.2 g per kg** body weight |
| Carbs | Rise meaningfully to fuel hard training and glycogen |
| Fats | Modest bump to support the surplus |

Powder convenience if useful: [/go/protein-bulk](/go/protein-bulk) · [/go/creatine-bulk](/go/creatine-bulk).

## Training for hypertrophy (actionable)

With more fuel (and, in culture talk, more recovery capacity), volume and progression can rise — still within CNS limits ([Safety](/blog/bodybuilding-blueprint-safety)).

- **Split examples:** 5-day Push / Pull / Legs / Upper / Lower, or Push / Pull / Legs / Arms rotation
- **Double progression:** Pick a rep range (e.g. 8–12). When you hit the top of the range on all sets with a load, add weight next session and rebuild from the bottom of the range
- **Cardio:** Drop weekly HIIT to protect recovery; keep **2–3 × ~20 min Zone 2** for heart health and partitioning

## Chemical talk in culture (literacy only)

After a cut + consolidation, enhanced forums often discuss **titrating** long-ester testosterone into higher weekly totals. **Common ranges people discuss** sit around **~300–400 mg/week**, sometimes split across more injections in the week to reduce oestrogen “spikes” in bro-science language.

### Oestrogen management & AI misuse

Culture often says “keep an aromatase inhibitor (e.g. anastrozole talk) on hand, only if sides appear” — itchy/sensitive nipples, emotional volatility, sudden severe water retention get named as triggers.

Harm-reduction literacy:

- **Blind AI use** (crashing oestrogen “just in case”) is a known way people feel terrible — joints, mood, lipids  
- Self-prescribing prescription medicines without lawful authority is a legal and medical problem  
- We don’t teach a DIY estrogen protocol  

If sides or labs worry you: **clinician**, not a fitness CTA. Peers for pathology access education (not affiliated): [RoidSafe](https://roidsafe.com.au). Product-quality culture literacy: [PED Test AU](https://pedtestaustralia.com/) — not a buy path for gear.
