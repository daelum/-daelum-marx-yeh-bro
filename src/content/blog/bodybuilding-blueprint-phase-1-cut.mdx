## Goal of this phase

Strip body fat while keeping training quality and lean tissue. A stable, leaner baseline is the foundation for any later surplus — natural or otherwise.

## Why “cut first” shows up in enhanced culture

Gym culture often argues that starting a muscle-building pharmacology phase at a **high body-fat percentage** increases **aromatisation** (conversion of testosterone toward oestrogenic pathways), with unwanted water retention and gynaecomastia risk discussed endlessly on forums.

Education take: that reasoning is why people sequence fat loss before escalation talk. It is **not** permission to self-prescribe. Medical decisions stay with a clinician. We don’t source gear.

## Training & nutrition (actionable)

### Nutrition — controlled deficit

| Lever | Practical target |
|---|---|
| Calories | About a **20% deficit** below estimated TDEE (track weight trend 2–3 weeks; adjust) |
| Protein | **2.2–2.5 g per kg** body weight — satiety + muscle retention in a deficit |
| Carbs | Bias most carbs around pre- and post-training for anaerobic sessions |
| Fats | Roughly **0.6–0.8 g per kg** for basic cellular / fat-soluble vitamin needs |

This is food maths, not medical dosing. If health conditions apply, ask a clinician or accredited dietitian.

### Training — keep the strength

- **Weights:** 4 days/week **Upper / Lower**. Keep strength metrics; don’t switch to “light forever” just because you’re dieting.
- **Zone 2 LISS:** ~30 minutes easy walking most mornings — sustainable NEAT, not punishment cardio.
- **HIIT (once weekly):** e.g. bike or hill — about **6–8 rounds** of ~10-second hard efforts with ~80 seconds full rest. One Goldilocks stimulus, not daily redline.

Empty-stomach morning walks are a **preference some people use**, not a magic fat-burn law. Consistency beats dogma.

## Chemical talk in culture (literacy only)

In enhanced forums, Phase-1 “baseline” chatter often names long esters (e.g. enanthate / cypionate) and **common ranges people discuss** around **~100–150 mg/week**, sometimes split across the week to chase stabler levels, framed as “high-normal replacement while dieting.”

| Frame | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Culture pattern | What gyms/forums talk about |
| Not a prescription | We are not writing your injection schedule |
| Harm reduction | Bloodwork and clinical care beat Discord protocols |

If someone is already using despite legal and health risk, literacy still points to **honesty with a clinician**, symptom watch, and [PED harm reduction](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia) — including non-affiliated peers like [RoidSafe](https://roidsafe.com.au) for pathology-referral pathways.

## Exit criteria before Phase 2

- Body-fat visibly down and weight trend stable enough to stop crashing calories
- Strength mostly preserved
- Ready to **raise calories to maintenance** and verify health markers — not jump straight into “blast” folklore
