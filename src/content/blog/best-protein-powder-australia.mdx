## Scoop maths (do this before any brand loyalty)

```
total protein grams ≈ protein_per_serve × serves
AUD per 25 g = (price_AUD ÷ total protein grams) × 25
```

Include shipping to your postcode when it’s not free.

## Label red flags AU buyers miss

- Scoop size games (tiny scoop → flashy “% protein”)
- Headline “30 g protein” that doesn’t match the panel
- Proprietary blends burying protein sources
- Sugar alcohols / sweeteners fine for many — just know what’s in the tub
- US DTC with “free shipping” that isn’t free to AU
- Before/after implying steroid-level change from powder alone — skip the brand

## How much protein? (general framing only)

Common sports-nutrition ranges for lifters often land around **~1.6–2.2 g/kg** body mass depending on goals, energy intake, and context. Powder fills gaps; it doesn’t replace food quality or progressive training. Individual needs vary — **not a personal prescription**.

## Who should buy / wait

**Buy now if:** you miss protein targets from food alone and you’ve picked a lane (WPC / WPI / plant) with honest maths.

**Wait if:** your training and sleep are chaos and you’re hoping powder fixes that — it won’t.

**Skip brands that:** sell hormone theatre, grey chemistry vibes, or “guaranteed shred” claims.

## FAQ

**Is Australian-made automatically better?**  
Not automatically — but shipping, returns, and AUD clarity often are. Compare panels either way.

**Concentrate or isolate for bulking?**  
Either works if daily protein lands. Concentrate usually wins on budget.

**Can women use these same tubs?**  
Yes. Ignore pink-tax packaging unless the product actually wins on maths/taste.

**Mass gainers?**  
Different product: more carbs/calories. Not the default “protein powder” pick here.
