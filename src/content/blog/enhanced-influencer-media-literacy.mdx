Some creators are enhanced. Some aren’t. Many won’t say. **Media literacy** means you stop treating every reel as a randomised controlled trial of “chicken, rice, and discipline.” Steal the useful cues (form, progressive patterns). Bin the timeline, the pharmacy cosplay, and the checkout panic.

## TL;DR

- Peak stills include lighting, pump, angles, edits — and sometimes pharmacology — [natty or not](/blog/natty-or-not-physique-standards).
- “Day in the life” content is entertainment. Your week needs [Yeh Bro System](/start-here) boredom.
- Affiliate links aren’t automatically evil; **affiliate-first programming** is — [coach red flags](/blog/online-coach-red-flags) · our [disclosure](/disclosure).
- Yeh Bro does not source AAS / SARMs / grey peptides — [PED hub](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia).

| Layer | What to remember |

| Training age | Years beat eight-week challenges — [ego lifting vs training age](/blog/ego-lifting-vs-training-age) |

Assuming everyone is on drugs is lazy. Assuming nobody is also lazy.

## What to steal from good creators
