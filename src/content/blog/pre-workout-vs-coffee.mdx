**Scope:** Legal sports nutrition · seed `/go/` only.

## TL;DR

- **Alertness only?** Coffee (or labelled caffeine) usually wins on **cost + control**.
- **Want citrulline / other actives without guessing?** Open-label pre — not a proprietary blend.
- **Evening sessions?** Protect sleep — lower stim or stim-free path; don’t ego-dose.
- Creatine daily still beats another fancy pre for most beginners.

Caffeine timing: [Caffeine dose for training](/blog/caffeine-dose-training) · Blends: [Proprietary blend pre-workout](/blog/proprietary-blend-pre-workout) · Stim-free: [Stim-free pre](/blog/best-stim-free-pre-workout-australia)

## Side-by-side

| Factor | Coffee | Labelled pre | Proprietary blend pre |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caffeine control | High | Medium–high if mg listed | Poor if hidden |
| Extra actives | No (unless you DIY) | Possible if labelled | Guessing |
| AUD / session | Often lowest | Medium | Medium–high + waste risk |
| Sleep risk | Timing-dependent | Timing + formula | Timing + surprises |
| Yeh Bro default | Strong for alertness | OK if open label | **Skip most** |

Illustrative AUD (check live): a café coffee or home brew often undercuts a $2–3 pre scoop when all you wanted was awake.

## FAQ

**Does coffee dehydrate me for training?**  
Normal coffee intake isn’t a reason to skip training hydration basics. Don’t overcomplicate it.

**Can I stack coffee + pre?**  
Only if you **add the mg** and still sleep. Many people accidentally double-stim.

**Is pre better for fat loss?**  
Don’t buy fat-burner medical claims. Diet + training do that job.

**Women?**  
Same control/sleep maths. No special “lady pre” required.
