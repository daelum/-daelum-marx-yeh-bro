## TL;DR

- **Loading is optional.** You don’t need it for creatine to work.
- Default for most: **3–5 g monohydrate daily** from day one.
- Optional load: ~**20 g/day split** for **5–7 days**, then **3–5 g** maintain.
- Load = faster saturation; also more chance of early gut noise / scale water drama.

## Loading maths (plain)

| Phase | Dose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Load (optional)** | ~20 g/day as **4 × ~5 g** | 5–7 days |
| **Maintain** | **3–5 g/day** | Ongoing |
| **No-load path** | **3–5 g/day** from day one | Ongoing (saturation over a few weeks for many) |

Miss a load day? Don’t “punish double” the next morning — resume the plan.

Not medical advice. Clinical concerns → professional first.

## Why most Yeh Bro readers skip the load

| Skip load if… | Consider load if… |
|---|---|
| You’re a beginner building the habit | You want saturation sooner and tolerate split doses |
| Big single scoops upset your gut | You’re fine splitting 4 serves |
| Scale/water anxiety is high week one | You understand water ≠ fat — [Does creatine cause bloating?](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating) |
| Budget/simplicity matters | You’ve already locked training + protein |

Loading does **not** unlock a different kind of gain. Same destination; different on-ramp speed.

## AUD note (illustrative)

A load week uses more powder up front (~100–140 g in 5–7 days) then drops to maintenance. A 1 kg mono tub still lasts months on 5 g/day. Fancy forms don’t make loading “required” — [Mono vs HCL](/blog/creatine-monohydrate-vs-hcl) · [Creapure vs generic](/blog/creapure-vs-generic-creatine).

## FAQ

**Is loading dangerous?**  
Split doses are the usual approach; gut comfort varies. This isn’t personal medical advice.

**Do women load differently?**  
Same optional framework — [Creatine for women](/blog/creatine-for-women).

**Can I load with coffee?**  
You can mix mono in coffee; loading still means multiple smaller serves across the day if you choose that path.

**What after a week off?**  
Resume 3–5 g daily; optional short load if you want faster re-saturation.
