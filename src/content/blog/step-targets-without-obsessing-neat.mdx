**NEAT** (non-exercise activity thermogenesis) is the calories you burn fidgeting, walking, standing — not the barbell session. It’s real. It’s also easy to turn into a second job with a step-counter personality. Here’s how to use steps as a **tool** without letting the ring own your mood.

## TL;DR

- Walking supports recovery, steps, and cut adherence — [walking guide](/blog/walking-for-lifters).
- Pick a **repeatable** daily range (e.g. 6–10k for many desk lifters), not a hero day then zero.
- On hard lower days, easy steps beat punishing HIIT “for NEAT.”
- If the watch spikes anxiety, hide the badge and keep the habit — [body dysmorphia adjacency](/blog/body-dysmorphia-adjacency-gym-culture) patterns apply to metrics too.

| Job | How steps help |

Steps are not progressive overload for your squat — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload).

## Setting a target without losing your mind

1. **Baseline a normal week** (no hero walks).  
2. Add **1–2k steps** if the average is very low — don’t jump to 15k overnight.  
3. Keep the target **flat across weekdays** you can defend.  
4. On brutal roster / sleep weeks, maintain a floor, not a PR — [late-shift sleep](/blog/sleep-hygiene-late-shift-lifters).  
5. Review weekly averages, not hourly guilt.

## Pairing with lifting days

| Day type | Step vibe |

## What obsession looks like

- Skipping dinner socials to hit a round number  
- Adding punishing finishers when the bar already took everything  
- Using missed steps as moral failure  
- Stacking stims to “earn” evening treadmills — [caffeine](/blog/caffeine-dose-training)
