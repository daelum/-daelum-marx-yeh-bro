Comment sections turn pharmacology into bloodsport. Accusations, fake confessions, and “gotcha” genetics debates burn hours you’ll never get back on the bar. Detox means **leaving the thread** and returning to process.

## TL;DR

- You will not jury a stranger’s hormones from a phone.  
- Accusation culture isn’t literacy — [natty or not](/blog/natty-or-not-physique-standards) · [influencer literacy](/blog/enhanced-influencer-media-literacy).  
- Floor etiquette: don’t harass people in person either — [etiquette](/blog/gym-etiquette-without-gatekeeping).  
- Spend the energy on [logbook](/blog/training-logbook) progress and [Start here](/start-here).

## Why debates feel addictive

| Hook | Reality |
|---|---|
| Moral superiority | Cheap dopamine |
| Detective cosplay | Filters and lighting exist — [photo lighting](/blog/photo-lighting-honesty-for-lifters) |
| Secret “research” tips in replies | Marketing/risk — [research-chem tells](/blog/research-chem-marketing-tells) |

## Detox rules

1. Mute keywords that pull you in.  
2. If you teach, talk **sets, food, sleep** — not witch hunts.  
3. Curiosity about PED risk → harm-reduction hubs, not comment lore — [PED hub](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia) · [forum lore vs clinical care](/blog/forum-cycle-lore-vs-clinical-care).  
4. Body-image spiral → step back — [body dysmorphia adjacency](/blog/body-dysmorphia-adjacency-gym-culture) · Lifeline **13 11 14** (AU).

## Soft close

Your PR isn’t in the replies. Close the app; open the logbook — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload).
