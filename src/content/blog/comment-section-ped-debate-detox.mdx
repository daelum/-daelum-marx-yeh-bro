**Scope:** Culture hygiene · companions [natty or not](/blog/natty-or-not-physique-standards) · [gym etiquette](/blog/gym-etiquette-without-gatekeeping) · [contest-prep culture](/blog/contest-prep-culture-vs-health).

Comment sections turn pharmacology into bloodsport. Accusations, fake confessions, and “gotcha” genetics debates burn hours you’ll never get back on the bar. Detox means **leaving the thread** and returning to process.

**Performance-enhancing drugs (PEDs)** here means the broader culture conversation around anabolic–androgenic steroids (AAS) and related compounds — literacy and harm reduction only. Yeh Bro does **not** source, dose, or coach cycles. Legal sports nutrition affiliates stay in their lane.

## TL;DR

- You will not jury a stranger’s hormones from a phone.
- Accusation culture isn’t literacy — [natty or not](/blog/natty-or-not-physique-standards) · [influencer literacy](/blog/enhanced-influencer-media-literacy).
- Floor etiquette: don’t harass people in person either — [etiquette](/blog/gym-etiquette-without-gatekeeping).
- Curiosity about risk → clinical care and harm-reduction hubs, not comment lore — [PED hub](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia).
- Spend the energy on [logbook](/blog/training-logbook) progress and [Yeh Bro System](/start-here).

## Opinion vs established fact

| Claim type | How to treat it |
|---|---|
| Lighting / pump / edits change photos | Ordinary photography — [photo lighting](/blog/photo-lighting-honesty-for-lifters) |
| “This influencer is definitely on X compound” from a still | **Opinion / guess** — usually unknowable |
| AAS and many PEDs carry real health risks | **Established risk literacy** — talk to clinicians; see [bloodwork literacy](/blog/ped-bloodwork-literacy) |
| Forum “research” tips in replies | Marketing and risk — [research-chem tells](/blog/research-chem-marketing-tells) · [forum lore vs clinical care](/blog/forum-cycle-lore-vs-clinical-care) |
| Your training still needs progressive overload either way | Practical training fact — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) |

Harm-reduction framing: if someone is already using or considering use, the useful move is **medical care, bloodwork literacy, and honest risk talk** — not a pile-on and not a DIY protocol from strangers. No sourcing. No dosing advice on this page.

## Why debates feel addictive

| Hook | Reality |
|---|---|
| Moral superiority | Cheap dopamine |
| Detective cosplay | Filters and lighting exist |
| Secret “research” tips in replies | Often engagement or grey-market marketing |
| Coach pile-ons | Often farming comments — [coach red flags](/blog/online-coach-red-flags) |
| Identity defence (“natty pride” wars) | Rarely improves your squat |

## Detox rules

1. Mute keywords and accounts that pull you into witch hunts.
2. If you teach online, talk **sets, food, sleep** — not hormone accusations.
3. Curiosity about PED risk → [PED harm reduction](/blog/ped-harm-reduction-australia) · [TRT vs AAS literacy](/blog/trt-culture-literacy-vs-aas) · [peptide literacy](/blog/peptide-literacy-harm-reduction) — not comment lore.
4. Body-image spiral → step back — [body dysmorphia adjacency](/blog/body-dysmorphia-adjacency-gym-culture). In Australia, Lifeline **13 11 14**.
5. In the gym: mind your session; don’t interrogate someone’s arms — [gym etiquette](/blog/gym-etiquette-without-gatekeeping).

## Common mistakes

- Treating comment certainty as evidence
- Harassing gym members based on internet vibes
- Using PED debates to avoid fixing sleep, protein, and progressive overload
- Collecting “research chemical” tips from replies (risk + marketing)
- Letting moral theatre replace clinician conversations when health is on the line

## Takeaways

Your PR isn’t in the replies. Close the app; open the logbook. Literacy over accusations; care over folklore — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) · [Yeh Bro System](/start-here).
