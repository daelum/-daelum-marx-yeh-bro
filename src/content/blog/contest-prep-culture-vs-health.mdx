Stage bodybuilding and “prep” content sell a look that is often **short-lived, dehydrated, and chemically assisted**. That craft can be real sport. It is not the same standard as “healthy athletic human in Tuesday lighting.” Confusing the two wrecks beginners and fuels grey-market shopping. This is literacy — **not** a peak-week water/carb protocol and not a sourcing guide.

## TL;DR

- Contest condition ≠ year-round health standard.  
- Feeds compress years of prep + variables into a still — [photo lighting honesty](/blog/photo-lighting-honesty-for-lifters) · [influencer literacy](/blog/enhanced-influencer-media-literacy).
