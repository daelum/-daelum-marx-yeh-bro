**Scope:** Budget buying · landed cost maths.

## TL;DR

- “Cheapest tub on the shelf” ≠ cheapest **protein**.
- Rank by **AUD per 25 g protein** after shipping.
- Keep a **quality floor**: clear panel, believable protein/scoop, AU-sane stockist.
- Default budget lane is usually **whey concentrate** — isolate costs more for a reason.
- Date-stamp winners; prices move weekly.

Method sibling: [Best protein powder Australia](/blog/best-protein-powder-australia) · WPC vs WPI: [Whey concentrate vs isolate](/blog/whey-concentrate-vs-isolate-australia)

## The only formula that matters

```
total protein grams ≈ protein_per_serve × serves
AUD per 25 g protein = (price_AUD_including_shipping ÷ total protein grams) × 25
```

Ignore “80 serves!” if each serve is 15 g protein. Do the panel maths.

## Cheap traps

- **Mass gainers** sold as “protein” — you’re buying carbs/calories
- **Single-serve sachets** — convenience tax destroys AUD / 25 g
- **US hype brands** with AU freight that doubles the real cost
- **Under-scooping** to make a tub last — you just miss protein targets
- **Stack filler**: five cheap aminos + weak whey > one decent WPC

## FAQ

**Is the cheapest always concentrate?**  
Usually for dairy users. Not always after shipping and panel tricks — calculate.

**Are supermarket own-brands OK?**  
If the panel and AUD / 25 g clear the quality floor, yes. Brand ego isn’t a macro.

**How often should I re-check prices?**  
Before each re-order. We date-stamp guides; retailers move weekly.

**Can I live on cheap protein powder only?**  
It’s a protein tool, not a full diet. Not meal-replacement medical advice.
