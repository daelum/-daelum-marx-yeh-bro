## TL;DR

- Vitamin D shows up in athlete/nutrition conversations — **we will not diagnose deficiency or treat disease** in this article.
- AU context includes **sunlight + food + optional testing via a clinician** — not “more gelcaps = more gains.”
- No therapeutic promises (bones-as-disease, immunity cures, depression treatment, hormone replacement cosplay).
- Prefer lab-guided decisions from a **qualified professional** when status is unclear.
- Yeh Bro still ranks **training → protein → creatine** above vitamin upsells.

## Hard compliance frame (AU / TGA-minded)

| OK | Not OK |
|---|---|
| “Vitamin D is a nutrient discussed in general nutrition” | “Cures osteoporosis / depression / COVID / autoimmune disease” |
| “Sun exposure and food contribute for many people” | “Skip the GP — just mega-dose” |
| “Supplements exist; labels vary” | “Guaranteed muscle / testosterone boost” |
| “Ask a professional about testing” | “Replaces prescribed care” |
| “Not medical advice” | Disease treatment advertising |

If you suspect low vitamin D or have a medical condition, **see a clinician**. This page will not interpret your bloodwork.

## Australia notes (practical, not clinical)

- AU latitude and lifestyle vary — desk workers vs outdoor workers aren’t the same context.
- “Australia = endless sun so nobody needs vitamin D” is a blunt myth; **we still won’t prescribe**.
- Food sources and fortified foods appear in general nutrition education — use public health resources and your clinician for personal advice.

## FAQ

**Should athletes all supplement vitamin D?**  
Not a blanket yes from us. Individual status varies — testing/advice belongs with professionals.

**Can I mega-dose because it’s “fat soluble insurance”?**  
More is not automatically better; excess can be a problem. Clinician guidance beats forum dosing.

**Women?**  
Same conservative frame. No “hormone balancing vitamin D” marketing from Yeh Bro.

**Does sunscreen mean I must supplement?**  
Sun-safety advice is public-health territory; personal supplement decisions stay with you + a professional.
