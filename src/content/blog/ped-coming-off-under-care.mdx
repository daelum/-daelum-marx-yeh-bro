Stopping is part of the culture story forums romanticise poorly. This piece is **care language**: what people underestimate when coming off — not a PCT shopping list and not a restart script.

## TL;DR

- Coming off can include **mood, motivation, libido, and strength** crashes. That’s a clinical conversation.
- “PCT” product marketing is not a substitute for lawful medical care.
- Plan **support**, training expectations, and honesty with a clinician — not a secret second blast.
- We don’t source gear, SERMs, or grey “PCT packs.”

## Why “just come off” is incomplete advice

Enhanced culture often treats exit as a checklist of drugs copied from a forum sticky. Reality:

- Endocrine recovery is individual
- Mental health can dip hard
- Body image pressure spikes when the pump and water look change
- Legal and supply issues don’t vanish because you decided to stop

Harm reduction means: **stop unsafe patterns under care**, monitor mood, and don’t use relapse into illegal supply as emotional regulation.

## What people underestimate

| Domain | Education point |
|---|---|
| Mood | Irritability, flatness, anxiety, or depressive symptoms can appear — seek help early |
| Training identity | You can keep lifting on a natural recovery plan — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) still applies |
| Physique expectations | Some of the look was water, glycogen, lighting — and sometimes pharmacology — [natty or not](/blog/natty-or-not-physique-standards) |
| Fertility / hormones | Conversations belong with clinicians, not Discord |
| Sleep / stress | Non-negotiable during transition — [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters) · [stress](/blog/stress-and-lifting) |

Suicidal ideation or crisis: emergency services or [Lifeline](https://www.lifeline.org.au/) (13 11 14 in Australia). ADF resources for substance concerns: [adf.org.au](https://adf.org.au/).

## Medical care beats product theatre

If someone used PEDs despite legal advice, coming off still starts with **healthcare**:

1. Be honest with a clinician about what was used (as best you know).
2. Discuss labs as *they* recommend — literacy: [bloodwork](/blog/ped-bloodwork-literacy).
3. Do not self-prescribe prescription medicines for “PCT cosplay.”
4. Treat supplement ads promising “hormone restart” with extreme scepticism.

Legal sports nutrition can remain normal training food — protein, creatine — without being rebranded as PCT. [Beginner stack](/blog/beginner-supplement-stack-australia) is for lifters, not exit pharmacology.

## Training while coming off

Keep the craft:

- Slightly lower volume if mood and sleep are rough — or a [deload](/blog/deload-week)
- Maintain skill on big lifts; ego maxes help nobody
- Walk daily — [walking](/blog/walking-for-lifters)
- Eat enough protein — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters)

The goal is a sustainable natural training identity, not a temporary punishment phase until the next cycle.

## What we refuse to publish

- PCT drug protocols as site voice
- Affiliate funnels for “on-cycle / PCT stacks”
- Sourced gear, UGL reviews, or “safer blast” tips
- Encouragement to start PEDs so you can practice coming off
