Cables aren’t cheating. Free weights aren’t purity. **Cables** give continuous tension and easy angle changes; **free weights** tax stability and transfer to strength sports. Most lifters should use both when available.

## TL;DR

- Hypertrophy / joint-friendly volume → cables and machines shine.  
- Strength skill on squat/hinge/press → free weights earn practice — [powerbuilding](/blog/powerbuilding-hybrid-week).  
- Progress either with load/reps at honest RIR — [RPE](/blog/rpe-reps-in-reserve).  
- Travel/hotel: mix cables + DBs — [travel gym](/blog/hotel-travel-gym-sessions).

## Trade-off table

| | Cables | Free weights |
|---|---|---|
| Tension | High through ROM | Varies with leverage |
| Stability demand | Lower | Higher |
| Micro-loading | Stack jumps vary | Plates often finer on barbell |
| Shoulder comfort | Often excellent for flyes/press angles | Barbell path can aggravate — [BB vs DB](/blog/barbell-vs-db-bench-shoulders) |
| Skill transfer | Specific | Broader for bar sports |

## Programming ideas

- Main strength lift free-weight → cable accessories.  
- Deload weeks: bias cables — [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style) · [injury return](/blog/injury-return-to-lift-mindset).  
- Home gym without cables: bands approximate some angles — [resistance bands](/go/bands-amz) · [home gym](/blog/home-gym-starter-minimalism).

Warm up either way — [warmup circuit](/blog/warmup-circuit-you-keep). Creatine still daily — [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).
