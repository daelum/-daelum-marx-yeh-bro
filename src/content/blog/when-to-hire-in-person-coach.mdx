**Scope:** Coaching decisions · [Start here · mindset](/start-here#step-1) · contrast [online coach red flags](/blog/online-coach-red-flags).

Online PDFs can be fine. An **in-person coach** earns their fee when they see your bar path, fix your hinge, and adjust the plan you actually follow. They’re optional — not a moral upgrade. Hire when the bottleneck is eyes-on-skill or accountability you can’t fake alone.

## TL;DR

- DIY first: run [Start here](/start-here) for 8–12 weeks with a [logbook](/blog/training-logbook).  
- Hire in-person for **technique**, injury-adjacent returns (with clinician clearance), or real accountability.  
- Still screen for red flags — shame, PED pressure, supplement carts as the program.  
- A good coach makes your week repeatable, not cinematic.

## Signs you’re ready for eyes-on help

| Signal | Why in-person helps |
|---|---|
| Lifts stall with honest effort | May be technique or programming — [plateau checklist](/blog/plateau-checklist-for-lifters) |
| Hinge/squat feel sketchy on video | Live cueing beats comment-section form checks — [hinges](/blog/joint-friendly-hinge-progressions) |
| You keep ghosting sessions | External accountability |
| Returning after pain (cleared) | Coached regressions |

## Signs you should wait

- Can’t hit three sessions on the easiest template — [full body](/blog/beginner-full-body-program)  
- Expect a coach to out-train zero sleep — [sleep](/blog/sleep-for-lifters)  
- Want them to design AAS/TRT protocols — that’s clinical/illegal-supply territory we refuse — [TRT vs AAS](/blog/trt-culture-literacy-vs-aas) · [research-chem tells](/blog/research-chem-marketing-tells)

## How to interview a floor coach

1. Ask how they progress novices — want [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) language, not random WODs.  
2. Ask about deloads — [deload styles](/blog/how-to-choose-deload-style).  
3. Watch a session: do they coach or scroll?  
4. Supplement talk should be optional basics — creatine/protein — not grey vials. Soft literacy: [Muscle Nation creatine monohydrate](/go/creatine-amz).  
5. Fee clarity; no hostage contracts without an exit.

Etiquette while sharing the floor: [gym etiquette](/blog/gym-etiquette-without-gatekeeping).
