**Refeed** and **diet break** get thrown around like stage-prep spells. For most people they’re simpler: planned periods where you eat *more* (often more carbohydrates) or return near maintenance so adherence, training, and hormones-of-stress don’t fall off a cliff. This is literacy for gym lifters — not a contest peak-week protocol and not a PED (performance-enhancing drug) playbook.

## TL;DR

- **Refeed:** short bump (often 1 day) — usually carbs up, protein steady, keep the week mostly in deficit.
- **Diet break:** longer stretch (often 1–2+ weeks) near estimated maintenance to reset adherence / training quality.
- They don’t “melt fat faster” by magic. They can make a cut **survivable** so you actually finish it.
- If hunger, sleep, and lifts are fine, you may not need either yet — [hunger noise vs need](/blog/hunger-on-a-cut).
- No pharmacology cosplay here — [forum lore vs clinical care](/blog/forum-cycle-lore-vs-clinical-care).

## Plain definitions

| Term | Gym meaning |
|---|---|
| **Refeed** | Planned higher intake day (often carb-focused) inside a cut |
| **Diet break** | Multi-day / multi-week return toward maintenance calories |
| **Cheat day** | Unplanned free-for-all — usually wrecks the weekly average |
| **Reverse diet** | Slow climb out of a deficit after the cut (related idea, separate article someday) |

A refeed is a tool. A weekend blackout is not a refeed.

## When a refeed earns the slot

| Signal | Maybe useful |
|---|---|
| Training flat for days despite sleep | Carb bump around sessions — also see [carbs around training](/blog/carbs-around-training) |
| Adherence cracking (you’re about to blow the week) | One planned high day beats seven secret ones |
| Very lean / long cut | Psychological + practical relief |

**Not** required every Wednesday because TikTok said so.

## When a diet break earns the slot

- Weeks of aggressive deficit with trash gym performance and mood
- Life stress stacked on the cut — [stress and lifting](/blog/stress-and-lifting)
- You’re “cutting” but the scale and the fridge are already chaotic — fix structure first — [meal structure without apps](/blog/meal-structure-without-apps)

Hold near maintenance, keep protein high, keep lifting — [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) doesn’t pause forever. Then resume a moderate deficit — [cutting guide](/blog/cutting-without-wrecking-lifts).

## Practical templates (not prescriptions)

### Simple refeed day

- Keep **protein** in your usual range — [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters)
- Raise **carbs** (rice, potato, fruit, oats) especially around training
- Don’t treat oils and desserts as unlimited just because the word “refeed” appeared
- Next day: back to the plan

### Simple diet break week

- Eat roughly at the intake that held your weight before the cut
- Keep steps and sessions — [walking](/blog/walking-for-lifters) · [rest days](/blog/rest-days-vs-active-recovery)
- Watch the average over 7 days, not Tuesday morning panic
