**Scope:** Australia-first label literacy · seed `/go/` only.

## TL;DR

- Read the **nutrition panel**, not the vibe.
- Hard skips: proprietary dose-hiding, front-claim ≠ panel, hormone/PCT/AAS cosplay, disease-cure marketing.
- Run **AUD per effective dose** (protein / creatine / EPA+DHA / elemental Mg).
- When unsure → food/training framing and a boring seed pick — not grey chemistry.

- Numbers you can audit
- AUD honesty including shipping
- No banned Yeh Bro claims

## FAQ

**Are all US brands bad?**  
No — but AU shipping/duties and claim culture still need a cold read.

**Is “clinically dosed” enough?**  
Only if the panel shows the doses.

**Women’s products?**  
Same flags. Pink tax isn’t a quality metric.
