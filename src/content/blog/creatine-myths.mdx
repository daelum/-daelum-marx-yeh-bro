**Scope:** Creatine literacy · legal sports nutrition · [Start here](/start-here) step 5.

Creatine monohydrate is boring in the best way. The myths are louder than the powder.

## TL;DR

- **3–5 g/day** monohydrate is enough for most — [how to dose](/blog/how-to-dose-creatine).
- Loading optional. Timing mostly habit. Cycling not required for most healthy lifters.
- Scale bumps are often **water in muscle**, not fat — [bloat explained](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating).
- If you have kidney disease or medical concerns, ask a clinician before using it.

## Myth → straight answer

| Myth | Straight answer |
|---|---|
| “You must load or it doesn’t work” | False. Loading speeds saturation; daily 3–5 g still works — [loading](/blog/creatine-loading-phase). |
| “Only post-workout” | Daily total > magical window — [timing](/blog/when-to-take-creatine). |
| “It makes you bald” | Strong causal panic isn’t settled science cosplay; if you’re worried, talk to a clinician — don’t invent forum law. |
| “It destroys kidneys in healthy people” | Healthy-lifter evidence base is reassuring at normal doses; **existing kidney disease** → clinician first. |
| “Women shouldn’t take it” | Same useful dose range for most — [creatine for women](/blog/creatine-for-women). |
| “You must cycle on/off” | No evidence-based need for most healthy users. |
| “HCL / buffered / ethyl ester is clearly superior” | Pay for monohydrate grams — [mono vs HCL](/blog/creatine-monohydrate-vs-hcl). |
| “Creapure is the only pure creatine” | Quality matters; paying extra is situational — [Creapure vs generic](/blog/creapure-vs-generic-creatine). |
| “It replaces training and protein” | No. It’s a support habit on top of [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) and [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters). |

## What creatine actually is in a stack

For average lifters building the best shape of their life:

1. Training consistency
2. Protein + calories appropriate to the phase
3. Sleep
4. **Then** creatine as the easy win

Order of operations: [beginner stack](/blog/beginner-supplement-stack-australia).
