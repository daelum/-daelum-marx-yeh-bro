**Scope:** Creatine literacy · legal sports nutrition · [Yeh Bro System](/start-here) step 5.

Creatine monohydrate is boring in the best way. The myths are louder than the powder. Most of what circulates in comment sections is recycled forum folklore — not a reason to skip one of the few supplements with a large, repeated evidence base in healthy lifters. This page is myth-busting, not medical advice. If you have kidney disease or other clinical concerns, ask a clinician before using it.

## TL;DR

- **3–5 g/day** monohydrate is enough for most — [how to dose](/blog/how-to-dose-creatine).
- Loading optional. Timing mostly habit. Cycling not required for most healthy lifters.
- Scale bumps are often **water in muscle**, not fat — [bloat explained](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating).
- Women are not excluded by default — [creatine for women](/blog/creatine-for-women).
- Older lifters are often *especially* interested candidates under normal health screens — [creatine for older lifters](/blog/creatine-for-older-lifters).

## Myth → straight answer

| Myth | Straight answer |
|---|---|
| “You must load or it doesn’t work” | False. Loading speeds saturation; daily 3–5 g still works — [loading](/blog/creatine-loading-phase). |
| “Only post-workout” | Daily total > magical window — [timing](/blog/when-to-take-creatine). |
| “It makes you bald” | Strong causal panic isn’t settled science cosplay; if you’re worried, talk to a clinician — don’t invent forum law. |
| “It destroys kidneys in healthy people” | Healthy-lifter evidence base is reassuring at normal doses; **existing kidney disease** → clinician first. |
| “Women shouldn’t take it” | Same useful dose range for most — [creatine for women](/blog/creatine-for-women). |
| “You must cycle on/off” | No evidence-based need for most healthy users. |
| “HCL / buffered / ethyl ester is clearly superior” | Pay for monohydrate grams — [mono vs HCL](/blog/creatine-monohydrate-vs-hcl). |
| “Creapure is the only pure creatine” | Quality matters; paying extra is situational — [Creapure vs generic](/blog/creapure-vs-generic-creatine). |
| “It replaces training and protein” | No. It’s a support habit on top of [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload) and [protein targets](/blog/protein-targets-for-lifters). |
| “You look soft / bloated so quit” | Often intramuscular water and glycogen context — [bloat](/blog/does-creatine-cause-bloating) · [scale panic](/blog/sodium-water-scale-panic). |

“Evidence base is reassuring” is not the same sentence as “safe for every human in every medical situation.” Personalise with a clinician when your history is not simple.

## What creatine actually is in a stack

For average lifters building the best shape of their life:

1. Training consistency
2. Protein + calories appropriate to the phase
3. Sleep
4. **Then** creatine as the easy win

Order of operations: [beginner stack](/blog/beginner-supplement-stack-australia). Creatine is legal sports nutrition. It is not a performance-enhancing drug (PED) protocol, not a “cycle,” and not a substitute for [Yeh Bro System](/start-here) basics.

Missed a day? Take the next dose and move on. You are saturating a reservoir over time, not casting a spell that breaks if the clock is wrong.

## Loading, timing, and caffeine panic

- **Loading** (higher short-term intake) is optional convenience for faster saturation — not a purity test — [loading phase](/blog/creatine-loading-phase).
- **Timing** is mostly about the dose you remember — [when to take creatine](/blog/when-to-take-creatine).
- **Coffee** in the same lifetime as creatine is fine for most people; you do not need a caffeine divorce to “activate” monohydrate.
- Mix in water, squash, or a protein shake — whatever makes adherence automatic.

If your gut hates big loading boluses, skip loading and stay on 3–5 g daily. Adherence beats heroic protocols.

## Buying without myth tax

Plain micronised monohydrate. Judge **cost per serve**, not flavour collabs or “next-gen” molecules with thin justification.

## Common mistakes

- Quitting after a 0.5–1 kg scale bump in week one
- Paying for exotic forms because a forum said monohydrate is “old”
- Stacking five creatine products and calling it optimisation
- Expecting creatine to fix missed sessions and low protein
